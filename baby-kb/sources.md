# Source Whitelist

Each source has a tier (1=authoritative, 2=evidence-based popular, 3=high-achievement practitioner, 4=logistics) and a one-line credibility note. KB files cite by short-key (e.g., `[CDC-MILE-2022]`) which maps to an entry here.

## Tier 1 — Authoritative

- **[CDC-MILE-2022]** CDC, "Learn the Signs. Act Early." developmental milestones, 2022 revision. https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/ — 75th-percentile thresholds; replaced 2004 50th-percentile list. Some SLPs criticize the shift; for surveillance the CDC checklist is still the working US standard.
- **[CDC-IMZ-2026]** CDC Child and Adolescent Immunization Schedule, 2026. https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-age.html — Adopted by AAP, AAFP, ACIP. Definitive US vaccine schedule.
- **[AAP-IMZ-2026]** AAP 2026 Immunization Schedule policy statement, Pediatrics. https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/doi/10.1542/peds.2025-075754 — AAP-endorsed; in 2026 differs from CDC on a few items (note inline if material).
- **[AAP-BFP]** AAP Bright Futures Periodicity Schedule. https://www.aap.org/en/practice-management/care-delivery-approaches/periodicity-schedule/ — Standard well-visit cadence.
- **[AAP-SAFESLEEP-2022]** AAP 2022 Safe Sleep Recommendations, Pediatrics 150(1). https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/150/1/e2022057990/188304/ — Back-Alone-Crib; explicit no-bedshare; room-share 6 mo.
- **[AAP-FEVER]** AAP Clinical Practice Guideline, Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8–60 Days Old, Pediatrics 2021. https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/148/2/e2021052228 — Definitive febrile-infant pathway.
- **[AAP-MEDIA-2016]** AAP Council on Communications and Media, "Media and Young Minds," Pediatrics 2016. — Source of the under-18-mo no-screens-except-video-chat guidance.
- **[CDC-SAFESLEEP]** CDC Safe Sleep / SUID program. https://www.cdc.gov/sids/ — Epidemiology and risk-factor data.
- **[ACOG-PPD]** ACOG Committee Opinion 757 (2018), "Screening for Perinatal Depression." — Universal screen at least once during perinatal period; use validated tool.
- **[NIH-LEAP]** Du Toit et al., "Randomized Trial of Peanut Consumption in Infants at Risk for Peanut Allergy" (LEAP), NEJM 2015. — Foundational evidence for early peanut introduction.
- **[NASEM-PEANUT]** NIAID 2017 Addendum Guidelines for prevention of peanut allergy. — Operationalizes LEAP for clinicians.

## Tier 2 — Evidence-based popular

- **[OSTER-CRIB]** Emily Oster, *Cribsheet* (2019). — Economist's review of parenting evidence. Clean on breastfeeding overclaims, sleep training, daycare. Use for tradeoff framing.
- **[OSTER-FF]** Emily Oster, *The Family Firm* (2021). — Frameworks for the school-age years; useful for activity-pipeline planning.
- **[OSTER-PD]** ParentData newsletter. https://parentdata.org — Ongoing updates from Oster, including 2024+ items not in the books.
- **[CAPLAN]** Bryan Caplan, *Selfish Reasons to Have More Kids* (2011). — Twin/adoption-study evidence that parenting style has smaller long-run effects than commonly believed. Useful counterweight to anxiety. Note: Caplan undersells SES/peer effects; treat as one data point.
- **[LIEBER]** Ron Lieber, *The Opposite of Spoiled* (2015). — Money/values frame for kids. Relevant from ~age 3+; useful for activity-pipeline philosophy.
- **[FEILER]** Bryan Feiler, *The Secrets of Happy Families* (2013). — Family rituals, decision-making frameworks.
- **[DRUCKERMAN]** Pamela Druckerman, *Bringing Up Bébé* (2012). — Sleep ("the pause"), eating, and self-regulation framing. Use selectively — anecdotal in places.
- **[KARP]** Harvey Karp, *The Happiest Baby on the Block* (2002). — 5 S's for newborn soothing. Operational, well-known to pediatricians, evidence base is moderate.
- **[WEISSBLUTH]** Marc Weissbluth, *Healthy Sleep Habits, Happy Child* (5th ed.). — Extinction sleep training; nap architecture by age. Use for nap windows; note the strict-extinction posture is more aggressive than Ferber.
- **[FERBER]** Richard Ferber, *Solve Your Child's Sleep Problems* (rev. 2006). — Graduated extinction (the "Ferber method"). Most cited evidence-based sleep program.
- **[MINDELL-2006]** Mindell et al., "Behavioral treatment of bedtime problems and night wakings in infants and young children," Sleep 2006. https://aasm.org/resources/practiceparameters/review_nightwakingschildren.pdf — AASM review; 14/14 graduated-extinction studies positive.
- **[HISCOCK-2012]** Hiscock et al., "Five-year follow-up of harms and benefits of behavioral infant sleep intervention," Pediatrics 2012. — No measurable harm from sleep training at 5-yr follow-up.

## Tier 3 — High-achievement-oriented practitioner

- **[SUZUKI]** Shinichi Suzuki, *Nurtured by Love* (rev. 1983). — Mother-tongue method philosophy. Formal lessons typically begin age 3–4; listening exposure begins at birth.
- **[SUZUKI-ASSN]** Suzuki Association of the Americas. https://suzukiassociation.org — Operational standards, teacher directory.
- **[DOMAN]** Glenn Doman, *How to Teach Your Baby to Read* (1964). — Evidence base is thin; cite only for cultural framing of intensive early literacy. Mark `[evidence: low]` when used.
- **[CHUA]** Amy Chua, *Battle Hymn of the Tiger Mother* (2011). — Cultural framing for high-expectation parenting. Use for posture, not protocol.
- **[REACH-OUT-AND-READ]** Reach Out and Read program; AAP-endorsed reading-aloud guidance. https://reachoutandread.org — Evidence base for dialogic reading.
- **[WORD-GAP]** Hart & Risley (1995); Sperry et al. replication 2019; Romeo et al., "Beyond the 30-Million-Word Gap," Psych Science 2018. — Conversational turns, not raw word count, predict language outcomes. Cite the Romeo nuance, not the original 30M figure as gospel.

## Tier 4 — Logistics

- **[WIRECUTTER-BABY]** NYT Wirecutter baby gear reviews. https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/baby/ — Product picks. Independent enough to trust; flag affiliate bias when relevant.
- **[BOGLEHEADS-529]** Bogleheads wiki, 529 plans. https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/529_plan — Cleanest community-vetted summary of state-by-state 529 mechanics.
- **[SAVINGFORCOLLEGE]** SavingForCollege.com. — State-by-state 529 plan rankings and contribution limits.
- **[VANGUARD-529]** Vanguard / Nevada 529 plan documentation. — One of the standard low-cost direct-sold plans.
- **[ISAAGNY]** Independent Schools Admissions Association of Greater NY. https://www.isaagny.org — Binding NYC private-school notification/reply dates.
- **[PARENTS-LEAGUE-NY]** Parents League of New York. https://www.parentsleague.org — Ground-truth for NYC preschool process.
- **[ISSFBA]** Independent Schools of San Francisco Bay Area. https://www.issfba.org — SF/Bay-equivalent of ISAAGNY.
- **[SFUSD-ENROLL]** SFUSD enrollment portal. https://www.sfusd.edu/schools/enroll — Public-school enrollment timing for SF.

## Explicitly excluded

The following are NOT to be cited and should not appear in renderer output, even if they surface in upstream search:
- William and Martha Sears (*The Baby Book*) and the attachment-parenting catalog. Reasoning: ideology over outcomes; some clinical claims unsupported.
- Janet Lansbury / RIE / Magda Gerber. Reasoning: parents explicitly excluded.
- "Natural" anti-formula advocacy (La Leche League is borderline — cite only on logistics, not on outcomes claims).
- Anti-vax content of any kind, including "delayed schedule" advocacy not endorsed by CDC/AAP.
- Mommy-blog content not backed by Tier 1–3 evidence.
- Wakefield, anything related, in any form.

## Citation conventions

In KB files, cite as `[CDC-MILE-2022]` or `[OSTER-CRIB, p.142]` if a page reference is meaningful. The renderer can elide page numbers in the email but the KB keeps them.
