# Week 50 — Days 343–349

## Focus
12-month visit final scheduling window. The visit covers the largest single vaccine round of year 1 (MMR, VAR, HepA, PCV #4, Hib booster). Anticipatory guidance shifts to year 2: speech expectations, table foods, dental, walking refinement.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks; getting more confident.
- **Cognitive**: uses tools to extend reach; imitates elaborate actions.
- **Language**: 1–5+ words; many gestures.
- **Social**: warm with familiar; clear stranger wariness.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Mild post-MMR fever and rash 7–12 days post-vaccine.
- `[monitor]` Persistent absence of milestones.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis post-vaccine; respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy unrelated to vaccine.

## Action items for parents
- **12-month visit attended** if not yet.
- **First dental visit** scheduled — AAP recommends by age 1 OR within 6 mo of first tooth.
- **Year-1 retrospective conversation**: with partner, with pediatrician, in the year-1 photo book.

- **Music**.
