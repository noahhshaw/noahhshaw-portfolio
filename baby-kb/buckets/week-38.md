# Week 38 — Days 259–265

## Focus
Receptive language is rich now — he likely understands 20–50 words and several simple commands. Production may include 1–3 specific words. Big variability is normal; the 12-mo CDC milestone for language is "calls a parent 'mama' or 'dada' or another special name."

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises; stands alone briefly; first few steps possible.
- **Cognitive**: looks for hidden objects; tries to use objects correctly.
- **Language**: 1–3 specific words; understands "no."
- **Social**: stranger anxiety usually easing slightly; preference for primary caregivers strong.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Toddler-like negotiation behaviors emerging (testing limits, pointing-and-protesting).
- `[monitor]` No babbling, no specific words by 12 mo (raise at 12-mo visit).
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; severe respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **Preschool tours active** — submit applications well before deadlines (ISAAGNY: by Jan 8).
- **Estate review**: revisit guardianship designation — does the named guardian still make sense? Annual review is best practice.
- **Pediatric dentist**: schedule first dental visit if not done — AAP recommends by age 1.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Books with single words on each page (*First 100 Words*) for explicit naming.
- **Cooking-together moments**: he sits in high chair while you prep dinner. Narrate; offer safe scraps to mouth.
- **Walking practice**: hand-held walking on outdoor surfaces.
