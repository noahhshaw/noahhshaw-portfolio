# Week 06 — Days 35–41

## Focus
The mother's 6-week postpartum visit typically falls this week. Expect OB clearance for exercise (if uncomplicated delivery), discussion of contraception, and a final EPDS screen. The baby's crying curve is at or just past peak. Many families describe week 6 as the first "this is sustainable" inflection point.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: more sustained tummy time tolerance (30–60 sec). Hands beginning to open more often.
- **Cognitive/sensory**: smiles spontaneously, especially at parents.
- **Language**: vowel coos consolidating; takes turns vocalizing in proto-conversations.
- **Social**: social smile on its own to get attention (an early CDC 4-mo milestone, but many infants reach it earlier)[1].

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Hiccups during/after feeds; wet sneezing; quiet startle on loud sounds.
- `[monitor]` No social smile by 8 weeks — flag at the 2-month visit.
- `[call within 24h]` Cluster of new symptoms: poor feeding + lethargy + decreased output. New murmur or blue color around lips during feeds.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F. Apnea. Seizure-like activity. Inconsolable crying + fever.

## Action items for parents
- **Mother's 6-week postpartum visit** — bring written questions: pelvic floor, mood, return to exercise, contraception, breastfeeding plan, return-to-work date.
- **Schedule baby's 2-month well visit** if not already on the books.
- **Order pumping supplies** if mother is returning to work and will pump. The double-electric pump is typically covered by insurance under ACA — order through insurer's DME provider.
- **Confirm daycare/nanny start date** matches return-to-work date with a 1–2 week buffer.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 30 minutes/day cumulative across multiple bouts. Use a baby-safe mirror at his eye level — face-tracking motivates head lifts.
- **Read aloud** with intentional pauses — read a line, look at him, pause, read the next line. The "serve and return" cadence is active even pre-verbal.
- **Outdoor exposure**: a 20–30 minute walk in the stroller most days. Vitamin D, parental cardiovascular recovery, and varied visual/auditory input. AAP recommends 400 IU/day vitamin D supplementation for exclusively/predominantly breastfed infants regardless of outdoor exposure[2].

## What's coming next
- **Week 07**: Crying tapering. Daycare/nanny planning final stretch if 12-wk leave.
- **Week 08**: 2-month well visit + first major vaccine round.
- **Week 09**: Post-vaccine fussiness 24–48h is normal.
