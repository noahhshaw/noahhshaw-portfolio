# Week 36 — Days 245–251

## Focus
First-step territory begins for early walkers (range 9–17 months; CDC walks-alone milestone moved to 15 mo in 2022 revision but this is the 75th-percentile mark, not "abnormal if later"). Cruising is fluid for early movers.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: stands alone briefly for some; cruising for early movers (CDC checkpoint for both: 12 months); some take 1–3 first steps.
- **Cognitive**: tries to use objects correctly (puts cup to mouth, phone to ear).
- **Language**: 1–2 specific words possible ("mama"/"dada" specifically referring to parent).
- **Social**: gives objects when asked; plays interactive games.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Increased falls; hand-mouth habit strong.
- `[monitor]` No cruising by 10 mo; no babbling with varied consonants.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Severe respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **First-walking shoes** (when he's outside on rough surfaces — barefoot indoors stays best). Wirecutter recommends Stride Rite Soft Motion or similar.
- **Holiday travel logistics** if applicable: book flights now if peak season. Window seat + lap infant is standard under 2 yr (free domestic; FAA-approved car seat in own seat is safer if you can spend the fare).
- **Preschool application research finalizing**: by end of week 36, you should have a short list of 5–10 schools to apply to. ISAAGNY apps typically due January 8 of the year prior to entry; ISSFBA varies.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Walking practice**: hold both his hands; let him step. Push toy (Radio Flyer Wagon, vintage push corn-popper) supports cruising.
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Begin "tell me…" — he points to images you name.
- **Music**: continue.
