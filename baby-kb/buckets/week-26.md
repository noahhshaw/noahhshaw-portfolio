# Week 26 — Days 175–181

## Focus
Half-year mark. The baby has more than doubled birth weight, sits independently, eats solids, recognizes parents reliably, and is on the cusp of mobility. Sleep is generally consolidated for sleep-trained or naturally good sleepers; for others, this is a reasonable second window for sleep training. Take stock.

## Expected developmental milestones (CDC 6-month checklist, full)
- **Social/emotional**: knows familiar people; likes to look at self in mirror; laughs.
- **Language**: takes turns making sounds with you; blows raspberries; makes squealing noises.
- **Cognitive**: puts things in mouth to explore; reaches to grab a toy he wants; closes lips to show he doesn't want more food.
- **Motor**: rolls tummy-to-back; pushes up with straight arms in tummy time; leans on hands for support when sitting[1].

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Drool surge ahead of first tooth; mouthing everything.
- `[monitor]` No babbling; no rolling either direction; persistent failure to bear weight on legs when held.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent fever; lethargy disproportionate.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; seizure activity; signs of severe respiratory distress (RSV season caution).

## Action items for parents
- **Mid-year recap**: compute calories, sleep average, milestone comprehension. Pediatrician discussion at 6-mo visit (if recent) covers this.
- **First dental visit**: AAP recommends first dental visit by age 1 OR within 6 mo of first tooth eruption (whichever comes first). Schedule for 9–12 mo.
- **Crawl-proofing**: outlet covers, stair gates ready (top + bottom of stairs), TV anchored to wall, low cabinets latched, toilet locks if open-door bathroom culture.
- **Whole foods expanding**: introduce a wide variety — herbs, gentle spices, varied textures. The 6–12 mo window is the broadest taste-acceptance window before the toddler-aged "neophobia" kicks in (~18–24 mo).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: continue 30+ min/day. Begin alternating between the bedtime book (same nightly) and "library" rotation books (varied).
- **Music**: introduce a percussion instrument he can hold — egg shaker, soft maracas. Rhythm production practice.
- **Floor time**: 60+ min/day floor play with toys and parent at his level.

## What's coming next
- **Week 27**: Crossing into second half. Crawling on the horizon.
- **Week 28**: Stranger anxiety often beginning.
- **Week 29**: Pre-9-month landmarks.
