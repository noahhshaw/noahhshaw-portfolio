# Week 18 — Days 119–125

## Focus
Mid-stretch between 4-month vaccines and 6-month milestones. Active solids prep window. If you're aiming for early peanut/egg introduction (especially if there's family history of atopy or your baby has eczema), the AAP/NIAID protocol allows starting at 4 mo with high-risk infants — discuss with your pediatrician at the next visit.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: tripod sitting briefly; rolling both directions for some (CDC rolls-tummy-to-back checkpoint: 6 months); reaches toward objects with one hand.
- **Cognitive**: watches where a toy falls (searching for out-of-sight objects is a 9-month CDC checkpoint); distinguishes parents from strangers.
- **Language**: babbling consonants ("ba," "da," "ga"); takes turns vocalizing.
- **Social**: anticipates being picked up; distress when caregiver leaves room (early separation reactions).

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Persistent need to chew/mouth objects; first signs of teething (drool, fussiness, gum-rubbing); no eruption yet.
- `[monitor]` Sleep stretches not consolidating despite 4–6 weeks past regression onset and stable environment.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent eczema worsening — high-risk allergen-introduction conversation needs to happen pre-introduction.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F; reduced output (<5 wet diapers/day).

## Action items for parents
- **Allergen-introduction plan finalized**. Decision tree:
  - Severe eczema or known egg allergy → consult pediatric allergist BEFORE peanut introduction.
  - Mild eczema or family history → discuss with pediatrician; introduction at 4–6 mo per LEAP-derived NIAID guidelines[1].
  - No risk factors → introduce around 6 mo with other solids.
- **Equipment ready**: high chair set up, bibs, pre-loaded spoons (NumNum brand or similar), suction-cup bowls. Wirecutter has tested options; the IKEA Antilop is widely-cited best value.
- **Plan first foods**: see `topics/introducing-solids.md`. Common starting foods: avocado, banana, sweet potato, oatmeal cereal (iron-fortified), well-cooked egg yolk.
- **First-birthday photographer**: book if not yet (top photographers in NYC/SF book 4–6 mo out for milestone sessions).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: organic at this point — he's flipping in and out.
- **Reading**: 20+ min/day cumulative. Mix board books with cloth/touch-and-feel books.
- **Music**: try a baby class if not yet — Music Together sessions cycle quarterly, registration windows are 4–6 weeks pre-class.

## What's coming next
- **Week 19**: Final pre-6-mo prep.
- **Week 20**: 6-month prep visit may happen now in some practices.
- **Week 21**: Starting to consider solids if going at 6 mo.
