# Week 10 — Days 63–69

## Focus
Many infants produce their first true laugh this week (range 10–14 weeks). The visible reciprocity of social interaction sharpens; the baby is recognizably a person now to anyone meeting him. Sleep is at its most predictable in the 8–14 week window for many — bank it.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: pushes up on forearms with chest off the floor; head turning more deliberately.
- **Cognitive**: looks at hands with interest; brings hands to mouth.
- **Language**: chuckles; cooing back-and-forth in extended turns.
- **Social**: laughs out loud (some infants — others wait until 14–16 weeks). Recognizes parents reliably.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Drooling profusely (salivary gland maturation, not necessarily teething — first teeth typically 6–10 mo). Hand-sucking. Non-painful spit-up.
- `[monitor]` No social smile by now: flag at next visit. Persistent head asymmetry (positional plagiocephaly) — pediatric PT or repositioning advice.
- `[call within 24h]` Decreased feeding 24h+; new color changes during feeds.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F rectal. Apnea. Seizure-like activity.

## Action items for parents
- **Trial daycare or nanny for full-day shadow** if return-to-work is in 2–4 weeks.
- **Pumping setup at work**: confirm room location, fridge, schedule with manager. Federal PUMP Act gives nursing employees reasonable break time + private (non-bathroom) space for up to 1 year postpartum.
- **Begin tracking sleep windows**: when was the last wake, when did he fall asleep, length of nap. By 12–14 weeks the pattern stabilizes enough to anchor a loose schedule. See `topics/naps-by-age.md`.
- **First photo session**: if booking a "100-day" or 3-month milestone shoot, week 12–14 is the window. Some families do monthly Instax stack photos against a fixed backdrop — 12 months of them in a frame is the clearest documentation of the year.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: continue 60+ min/day cumulative. Try varying the surface — firm mat, parent's lap, slight incline on a Boppy.
- **Reading**: introduce one new book per week alongside the rotation of repeats. The repeat-vs-novel ratio of ~3:1 supports both prosodic familiarity and lexical novelty.
- **Sensory exploration**: textured toys (silicone teether, soft fabric book, smooth wooden ring). Vary surfaces. Avoid sensory bins with small loose pieces — choking risk too high at this age.

## What's coming next
- **Week 11**: Sleep often at its most consolidated — pre-regression.
- **Week 12**: First "almost-rolling" attempts; many parents notice the rotational core engagement.
- **Week 13**: Crossing into the second tertile of year 1; 4-month regression on the horizon.
