# Week 24 — Days 161–167

## Focus
Solids progression. By the end of this week most "starter" infants have tried 5–10 foods. The protocol question now is allergen-introduction frequency: introduction is not enough — sustained 2–3× weekly exposure maintains tolerance per LEAP and EAT findings.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits steadily; reaches with one hand; transfers objects between hands.
- **Cognitive**: brings hand to mouth deliberately for self-feeding.
- **Language**: babble strings with varied consonants.
- **Social**: smiles at self in mirror; laughs in social play.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Color/consistency change in stool with solids — totally normal. Some foods (beets, blueberries) pass through visibly.
- `[monitor]` Persistent constipation > 3 days; gagging frequently (vs. choking — gagging is a protective reflex, choking is silent and emergent).
- `[call within 24h]` Hives or vomiting after a new food.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis (facial swelling, breathing difficulty, hypotonia, color change, vomiting + hives within 30 min of food). Choking with no air movement.

## Action items for parents
- **Allergen frequency calendar**: aim for peanut, egg, dairy, wheat, soy each at least 2× per week from introduction onward[1].
- **Refresh CPR/choking knowledge**: AAP Heimlich-equivalent for infants is back blows + chest thrusts (NOT abdominal). Take an infant CPR class if not done; American Red Cross runs them weekly in most cities.
- **Stock acetaminophen and ibuprofen**: ibuprofen approved from 6 mo; acetaminophen weight-based dosing chart printed and posted (kitchen + diaper bag).
- **First photo of solids** for the family album / proverbial "first taste of avocado" document.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Family meals**: even 1 meal/day with the family at the table beats solo high-chair time for language exposure.
- **Reading**: 30 min/day. Try a "lift-the-flap" book — fine motor and anticipation.
- **Standing practice**: hold his hands and let him bear weight — strengthens leg muscles for eventual cruising.

- **Week 26**: Half-year birthday. Mid-year progress check.
- **Week 27**: Crossing into the second half.
