# Week 31 — Days 210–216

## Focus
Cruising consolidation. The first instances of "cruising" — stepping sideways while holding furniture — typically emerge 8–11 months. Vocal turn-taking is increasingly conversational in pattern, even though words proper haven't arrived.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises along furniture; gets to sitting from prone position.
- **Cognitive**: imitates simple actions (clapping, banging).
- **Language**: babble with intonation; "mama"/"dada" inconsistently.
- **Social**: clings to primary caregivers in unfamiliar settings.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Falls during cruising — head bumps unavoidable. Soft padding on sharp furniture corners.
- `[monitor]` Persistent inability to bear weight on legs; persistent absence of imitation.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent fever; new wheezing.
- `[call now]` Head injury with loss of consciousness, persistent vomiting after a fall, or unequal pupils — ER. Otherwise fall-related bumps with normal behavior afterward → monitor.

## Action items for parents
- **Furniture audit**: anchor everything tall. TV anchored. Bookshelves anchored. Dressers anchored. Tip-over deaths in toddlers are preventable.
- **Travel logistics if planning a trip** before 9-mo visit: passport applications take 6–12 weeks for infants (in person, both parents present, two photos).
- **First-tooth photo** if it's appeared (typical eruption 6–10 mo).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Begin asking "what's that?" with longer pauses.
- **Imitation games**: clap, wave, blow kiss. Imitation drives motor program acquisition.
- **First "playdate" structure** if doing — 30 min parallel play with 1 other baby.
