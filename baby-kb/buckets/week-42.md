# Week 42 — Days 287–293

## Focus
11-month informal landmark. The shift from "infant" to "toddler" is happening now (toddler = able to toddle). Receptive vocabulary likely 50–100 words; expressive 0–10 words.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks 3–10 steps for many; pulls to stand and walks holding furniture for nearly all.
- **Cognitive**: imitates familiar actions; uses objects with intent (hairbrush, phone).
- **Language**: 1–5 specific words; understands several simple commands.
- **Social**: peek-a-boo / pat-a-cake reliably; gives object on request.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Separation distress at daycare drop-off; bedtime resistance returning briefly.
- `[monitor]` No babble; no joint attention; no interest in shared games.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Severe respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **12-month visit scheduling** (window day 364–380). Major vaccines: MMR, VAR, HepA, PCV #4, Hib booster.
- **Pre-12-mo visit notes**: vocabulary list, sleep status, eating range, motor checkpoints.
- **Whole-milk transition** mostly complete by visit if doing.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day; introduce simple narrative arcs.
- **Walking practice**: extended outdoor walks.
- **Bilingual exposure**: by 12 mo, the second-language parent has provided ~9 mo of consistent input. Phoneme discrimination for both languages preserved if so[1].
