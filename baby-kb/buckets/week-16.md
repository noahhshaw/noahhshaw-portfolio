# Week 16 — Days 105–111

## Focus
4-month well visit window opens. This is a major milestone-tracking visit — the pediatrician will run through the full CDC 4-month checklist and administer the second major vaccine round (RV #2, DTaP #2, Hib #2, PCV #2, IPV #2). Sleep-regression discussion is on the agenda whether you raise it or not.

## Expected developmental milestones (CDC 4-month checklist)
- **Social/emotional**: smiles on his own to get attention; chuckles when you try to make him laugh; looks at you, moves, or makes sounds to get/keep your attention.
- **Language**: makes sounds like "oooo," "aahh" (cooing); makes sounds back when you talk to him; turns head toward sound of your voice.
- **Cognitive**: opens mouth when he sees breast/bottle if hungry; looks at his hands with interest.
- **Motor**: holds head steady without support when held; holds a toy when placed in his hand; uses arm to swing at toys; brings hands to mouth; pushes up onto elbows/forearms when on tummy[1].

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Drooling extensively — first teeth typically erupt 6–10 mo, but salivary maturation precedes them by 2–3 mo.
- `[monitor]` Persistent head asymmetry / flat spot — pediatrician may discuss helmet referral around 6 mo if not improving.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent vomiting; new-onset feeding refusal; injection-site reaction with hard knot >2".
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F at 90+ days; signs of anaphylaxis post-vaccine; seizure activity.

## Action items for parents
- **4-month well visit attended.** Suggested questions:
  - Should we be planning solids at 4 mo (early-introduction protocol, especially for high-risk allergens) or at 6 mo?
  - Sleep regression: any behavioral interventions you'd recommend?
  - Growth percentile trajectory — is he tracking?
  - Iron status — formula has 12 mg/L; exclusively breastfed infants begin to deplete stores around 6 mo.
- **Vaccine prep**: acetaminophen weight-based dose post-vaccine (avoid prophylactic dosing — blunts antibody response).
- **Childcare illness contingency tested by now** — record what worked, what didn't.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: maintain 60+ min/day even though he may roll out of it.
- **Reading**: dialogic reading begins to be tractable — "Where's the dog? Yes, the dog. The dog is brown."
- **Solids prep**: even if you're aiming for 6 mo, this is the week to research baby-led-weaning vs. purée philosophy. See `topics/introducing-solids.md`. Order high chair (the IKEA Antilop is the Wirecutter pick — $25, dishwasher-safe) and a few starter spoons/bibs.

## What's coming next
- **Week 17**: Post-vaccine recovery; sleep often improving.
- **Week 18**: Solids planning intensifying.
- **Week 19**: Final approach to 6-month milestone changes.
