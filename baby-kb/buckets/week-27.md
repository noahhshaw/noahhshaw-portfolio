# Week 27 — Days 182–188

## Focus
Second half of year 1 begins. Mobility transition window: most infants commando-crawl, scoot, or roll-roll-roll between 6 and 8 months; quadrupedal crawling typical 7–10 months; some skip crawling entirely. This is also early stranger-anxiety territory.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits without support reliably; pushes to all fours; some commando-crawling.
- **Cognitive**: looks for objects partially hidden; manipulates objects with both hands.
- **Language**: babble with intonation; "mamama," "dadada" (still non-referential).
- **Social**: reacts when caregiver leaves the room; responds to his name.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Stranger anxiety beginning (peaks 7–10 mo). Brief separation distress.
- `[monitor]` Persistent flat head despite repositioning — pediatrician may discuss helmet referral around 7–9 mo if no improvement.
- `[call within 24h]` Acute illness onset; persistent feeding refusal.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress (signs: nasal flaring, retractions, RR >60 at rest).

## Action items for parents
- **Babyproof actively** as crawling becomes imminent. Critical: outlet covers, stair gates, cabinet locks, TV anchors, blind cord cleats (strangulation hazard — every cord must be inaccessible).
- **Begin formal preschool research** if NYC/SF — top 2s programs notify in February of the year your child enters; the application year before that needs to start now (research, tour calendar, recommendations). See `schools/preschool-application-timeline.md`.
- **Toy rotation system**: have 10–15 toys; cycle 4–6 visible at a time. Reduces overstimulation, increases novelty effect.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30 min/day cumulative. Begin pointing-and-naming as a routine — "Where's the dog? *There's* the dog."
- **Music**: handheld percussion + your voice. Vary rhythm explicitly; give him a turn to make a beat.
- **Cause-and-effect**: shape sorter (without expectation of correct sorting), pop-up box, rolling balls.

- **Week 29**: Pre-9-mo prep.
- **Week 30**: Crawling for many.
