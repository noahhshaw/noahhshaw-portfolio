# Week 30 — Days 203–209

## Focus
Cruising precursor. Pulls to stand for many infants now. The motor surge often coincides with the 8/9-month sleep regression — separation-anxiety cognition + mobility excitement = night wakings to practice the new skill.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: pulls to stand at furniture; some cruise-step (stepping sideways while holding furniture).
- **Cognitive**: looks for fully hidden object; explores objects through banging, dropping, mouthing.
- **Language**: "mamamama" / "bababababa" varied babble.
- **Social**: stranger anxiety persisting; reaches for primary caregivers.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Standing in crib at sleep time, then crying because he can't get back down. Practice during day = teach to sit back down.
- `[monitor]` Persistent inability to crawl, scoot, or progress motorically.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness > 3 days.
- `[call now]` Respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **Stair gates** installed top + bottom. Hardware-mounted (not pressure-mounted) at top of stairs.
- **Crib mattress lowered** if he's pulling to stand — dropped to lowest setting.
- **Begin Suzuki/instrument lessons research** if planning age 3–4 start. Top NYC and SF Suzuki teachers have multi-year waitlists; getting on the list now (even though lessons start in ~2.5 years) is the right move.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Pause longer for him to "answer" questions.
- **Crawling games**: chase, tag, peek-a-boo with crawling.
- **Music class**: continue weekly attendance if doing.
