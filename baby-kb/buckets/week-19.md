# Week 19 — Days 126–132

## Focus
Pre-solids week. Sleep should be largely stabilized post-regression. The baby is on the cusp of sitting, mouthing-to-explore, and motor-driven exploration.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sitting with hands forward (tripod) for 5–10 sec; rolling possible/emerging (CDC 2022 checkpoint: 6 months); rocking on hands and knees for a few early movers (more typically ~6–10 months).
- **Cognitive**: brings objects to mouth deliberately; explores texture by mouthing.
- **Language**: babble strings; vocal play with intonation.
- **Social**: laughs at peek-a-boo; reaches arms to be picked up.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Putting everything in mouth — necessary developmental exploration. Choking-hazard audit overdue if not done.
- `[monitor]` Persistent inability to bear weight on legs when held standing; persistent inability to roll.
- `[call within 24h]` Acute illness onset.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F; sudden-onset lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **Choking-hazard audit**: walk every room at floor level. Coins, batteries, magnets (especially neodymium/Buckyballs — life-threatening if multiple swallowed), small toy parts, dog/cat food. See `topics/choking-hazards.md`.
- **Outlet covers / stove-knob covers** — install before crawling onset (typical 7–10 mo).
- **6-month visit prep**: bring list. Iron-supplement question if exclusively breastfed. Allergen plan. Sleep status.
- **Solids shopping**: simple food processor or immersion blender; small portion-size cubes; feeding-station setup.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Sitting practice**: prop him in a Boppy with toys around; floor-time with you behind him as a backstop.
- **Reading**: 20+ min/day. Begin to point and ask "where's…?" — pre-cursor to receptive language testing.
- **Hand-to-hand transfer practice**: offer a toy with one hand at midline; observe whether he takes it with one hand and switches to the other.
