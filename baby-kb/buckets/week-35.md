# Week 35 — Days 238–244

## Focus
Post-9-month-visit review. The mobility surge is in full force. Receptive language is developing rapidly: he likely understands 10–20 simple words even if he's not yet producing them. Production typically lags reception by 1–3 months.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises confidently; first independent stands (briefly) possible.
- **Cognitive**: anticipates routines; understands simple commands ("come here," "no").
- **Language**: imitates sounds; "mama"/"dada" specific to a parent emerging.
- **Social**: peek-a-boo; pat-a-cake emerging for some (a CDC 1-year item); turns head when name called.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Falls during cruising; bumps to head if walking surface is hard.
- `[monitor]` No cruising or pulling-to-stand; no response to name; no babbling with consonants.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; severe respiratory distress; head injury with persistent vomiting or lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **First-birthday planning**: 4 months out. Venue locked. Theme decided. Photographer confirmed.
- **Top NYC/SF preschool 2s applications**: research the 5–10 schools you'd consider; tour calendars typically published in fall (September).
- **Holiday season planning** (Halloween, Thanksgiving, December holidays). First holiday with infant — plan for early bedtimes, sleep environment continuity.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Begin asking "where's…" with multiple choices ("where's the dog? Is it here? Is it there?").
- **Songs with hand motions**: "Itsy Bitsy Spider," "Twinkle Twinkle." Imitation + rhythm + receptive vocabulary all in one.
- **Free play with blocks**: large, soft, washable. Stacking is too advanced (12–15 mo); knocking down is the active enjoyment.

## What's coming next
- **Week 36**: First steps possible.
- **Week 37**: Pincer grasp refining.
- **Week 38**: Pre-10-month informal milestone.
