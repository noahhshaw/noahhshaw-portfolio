# Week 22 — Days 147–153

## Focus
6-month visit prep. Solids countdown. The baby's social world is widening — he's noticing strangers, distinguishing parents, and his preference for primary attachment figures is sharpening (precursor to stranger anxiety, which peaks 7–10 mo).

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits independently for longer stretches; rolls deliberately to reach toys.
- **Cognitive**: explores objects with hands and mouth; brings toys to mouth, examines, mouths again.
- **Language**: takes turns making sounds (CDC 6-mo); blows raspberries; squeals.
- **Social**: knows familiar people; likes to look at self in mirror.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Increased clinginess; protests at being put down.
- `[monitor]` Lack of interest in toys; minimal vocalizing; failure to make eye contact.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness symptoms; new ear pulling + fever.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; seizure-like activity.

## Action items for parents
- **6-month visit prep list**:
  - Allergen-introduction plan reviewed with pediatrician.
  - Iron-status check (hemoglobin) — universal at 6–9 mo per AAP.
  - Lead exposure questionnaire.
  - Solids start strategy.
  - Sleep status (ongoing or new issues).
- **Iron-fortified cereal stocked** (Earth's Best, Gerber, Beech-Nut — single-grain oat is the standard starting point).
- **First-spoon plan**: if BLW, no spoon yet — soft strips of avocado/banana/cooked sweet potato; if purée, NumNum or similar GOOtensil.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: continue. Begin pointing and naming consistently — "this is a *cat*. The *cat* is on the *mat*."
- **Music**: Suzuki listening (if doing) plus live singing.
- **Floor play with parent below**: sit him on the floor; lie down beside him at his eye level. Eye-level interaction increases turn-taking quality.

- **Week 23**: 6-month visit possibly this week.
- **Week 24**: Visit + first solids.
- **Week 25**: Solids progression.
