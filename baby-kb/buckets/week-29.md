# Week 29 — Days 196–202

## Focus
Approaching 9-month visit. Crawling beginning for many; pulling-to-stand often emerges 8–10 months. Standing-cruise progression is in early stages.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: crawls (commando or quadrupedal); pulls to kneel or stand using furniture.
- **Cognitive**: looks for hidden objects; bangs two objects together (CDC 9-mo).
- **Language**: babbling getting richer; some imitation of intonation.
- **Social**: stranger anxiety; preference for familiar caregivers; lifts arms to be picked up (CDC 9-mo).

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Falling backward when sitting; falling forward when crawling — bumps and bonks normal.
- `[monitor]` Persistent inability to bear weight standing; persistent failure to bang two objects together.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent vomiting; new wheezing (RSV season).
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress; severe dehydration (no wet diaper >12h).

## Action items for parents
- **Schedule 9-month visit** if not done — window day 252–280, but pediatricians vary.
- **Babyproof completed**: walk through with the baby's perspective at floor level. Any small object he can pick up will go in his mouth. Houseplants (some toxic — check ASPCA list), liquor cabinets, drawer locks, fireplace surrounds.
- **Tax planning** for the year: dependent claim, dependent care FSA (use-it-or-lose-it), HSA contributions if eligible.
- **Plan first family trip with baby**: travel between 6–9 mo is often the practical sweet spot — pre-mobility, post-vaccines, pre-stranger-anxiety-peak. See `topics/travel-with-infant.md`.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Begin "what's on this page?" inventory style.
- **Banging music**: hand him a wooden spoon and a metal pot. Auditory feedback drives repetition.
- **Standing practice**: support him at hips while he stands at a low table.
