# Week 44 — Days 301–307

## Focus
Mid-quarter pre-birthday. Walking is the dominant motor question; first words are starting to consolidate. Sleep is generally well-established for sleep-trained babies; ongoing fragmentation is the renew-conversation-with-pediatrician moment.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walking emerging; standing alone reliably.
- **Cognitive**: imitates familiar actions; uses objects intentionally.
- **Language**: 1–5 specific words; understands "no" (CDC 12-mo).
- **Social**: gives objects on request; plays interactive games.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Falling during walking; bumps and bruises.
- `[monitor]` No interest in interactive games; no babble or words.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness; new ear infection signs.
- `[call now]` Head injury with concerning features (vomiting, lethargy, unequal pupils); fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **First-birthday decor / cake / smash-cake confirmed.**
- **Photographer confirmed for the day.**
- **Family RSVPs final.**

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: continue.
- **Walking outdoors**: 20–30 min walks he leads.
- **First "drawing": large crayon on butcher paper. Mostly mouthing, some marks.
