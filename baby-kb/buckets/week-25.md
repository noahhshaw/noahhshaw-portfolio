# Week 25 — Days 168–174

## Focus
Solids variety expanding to 10–15 foods. Begin offering a sippy cup of water (a few sips at meals) — AAP recommends introduction of an open or straw cup around 6 mo, full transition off the bottle by 12–18 mo[1]. Cup skill is also a fine-motor enrichment.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits independently for some (CDC sits-without-support checkpoint: 9 months); rocking on hands and knees emerging; some infants beginning to scoot or commando-crawl.
- **Cognitive**: closes lips to refuse food (a real signal — respect it).
- **Language**: "mamamama" or "bababababa" babble strings (CDC 9-mo precursor).
- **Social**: knows familiar people; reacts when caregiver leaves room.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Variable food acceptance day-to-day — expect 5–10+ exposures before liking a new food.
- `[monitor]` Persistent food refusal across categories; weight stagnation since starting solids.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent diarrhea > 3 days; new wheezing or persistent cough.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis; choking; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **Sippy/straw cup introduced** — open cup is best for oral-motor development; straw cup is acceptable; "sippy" cups (spouted) are tolerated but the AAP and most speech-language pathologists prefer open or straw.
- **Continue allergen rotation** 2–3× weekly each.
- **Photography milestone**: 6-month sit-up shot — solo on a blanket, tripod sit. Same backdrop as 100-day for a comparison stack.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day cumulative. Begin reading the same book at the same time daily (e.g., bedtime book) to anchor evening routine.
- **Music**: layer a "morning" song and "bedtime" song. The audio cue to time-of-day supports circadian entrainment.
- **Crawl-prep**: place toys at increasing distance during tummy time. Motor crawl precursor (pivoting → commando → quadrupedal) is endogenous, but practice opportunity matters.

## What's coming next
- **Week 26**: Half-year birthday. Mid-year recap.
- **Week 27**: Beginning to commando-crawl for some.
- **Week 28**: Stranger anxiety beginning for some.
