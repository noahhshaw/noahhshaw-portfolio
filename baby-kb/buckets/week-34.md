# Week 34 — Days 231–237

## Focus
9-month visit window typically opens. Major developmental surveillance check. The pediatrician will run through CDC milestones, do hemoglobin and possibly lead screen, and discuss the approach to 12 months.

## Expected developmental milestones (CDC 9-month checklist)
- **Social/emotional**: shy/clingy/fearful around strangers; shows several facial expressions; looks when you call his name; reacts when you leave; smiles or laughs when you play peek-a-boo.
- **Language**: makes a lot of different sounds like "mamamama"/"bababababa"; lifts arms to be picked up.
- **Cognitive**: looks for objects when dropped out of sight; bangs two things together.
- **Motor**: gets to a sitting position by himself; moves things from one hand to the other; rakes food with fingers; sits without support[1].

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Stranger anxiety; clinginess; brief illnesses from daycare circulation.
- `[monitor]` No babbling; no response to name; no joint attention; not transferring objects.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness; new wheezing.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **9-mo visit attended.** Vaccines: Influenza #2 if first season. No major vaccines unless catch-up.
- **Hemoglobin result**: <11 g/dL is iron deficiency; pediatrician will recommend iron supplement or dietary changes.
- **Standardized developmental screen** (ASQ-3 or similar) often run at this visit.
- **Tax check**: dependent care FSA usage if applicable; HSA contributions tracking.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Pause longer; respond to his vocalizations as words.
- **Object permanence games**: hide a toy under a cup, then show — predictable surprise.
- **First-walking-shoes**: not yet needed (barefoot is best for early walking development), but soft-sole shoes for outdoor walks once cruising starts.

## What's coming next
- **Week 35**: Post-visit; cruising consolidating.
- **Week 36**: First steps possible (range 9–17 mo).
- **Week 37**: Pincer grasp refining.
