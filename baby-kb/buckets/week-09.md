# Week 09 — Days 56–62

## Focus
Post-2-month-vaccine recovery week for most infants. This is also the start of "the easy stretch" between the colic peak (week 6) and the 4-month sleep regression (week 14–18). Use it. Sleep is more predictable, the baby is engaging more, and your operational systems are tuned.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: head up at 45° during tummy time; pushing up on forearms.
- **Cognitive/sensory**: tracks objects fluidly; responds to his own name with attention.
- **Language**: cooing back-and-forth conversations; vowel + consonant combinations starting ("goo," "ah-goo").
- **Social**: smiling reliably at familiar faces; cooing more; first tentative laughs may emerge (typical range 10–14 weeks).

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Lingering low-grade fussiness 24–72h post-vaccine. Spit-up volume decreasing as upper esophageal sphincter matures.
- `[monitor]` Asymmetric movement (favoring one side); persistent head turn to one side suggesting torticollis.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent vomiting unrelated to vaccines. New murmur. Output drop.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F rectal more than 72h post-vaccine — should be evaluated as a non-vaccine fever in this age.

## Action items for parents
- **Confirm next vaccines on calendar**: 4-month visit window opens day 112–126 (week 16–18). Vaccines: RV #2, DTaP #2, Hib #2, PCV #2, IPV #2.
- **If returning to work**: this is the last full week before the typical 12-week US leave ends. Trial-run daycare/nanny for 2–4h windows to test the handoff.
- **Estate planning, if not done**: revocable trust + pour-over will + guardianship designation. See `dad/estate-guardianship.md`. The named guardian should know they're named.
- **Begin researching first-birthday venue/format**: top NYC and SF venues book 6–9 months out. Even if you do a low-key family party, putting the date on the calendar now prevents conflict-collisions with your spouse's parents' visit.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time** 60+ min/day cumulative. Place toys just out of reach to motivate stretching.
- **Read aloud**: graduate from purely high-contrast books to simple narrative ("That's Not My Puppy" series, "The Very Hungry Caterpillar"). Texture books begin to engage tactile attention.
- **Live music**: try varied tempos — a slow lullaby, a fast clapping song, then back to slow. Tempo discrimination is sharpening; varied input preserves perceptual range[1].

## What's coming next
- **Week 10**: First laugh likely (range 10–14 weeks).
- **Week 11**: Sleep stretches may lengthen further — bedtime routine becomes more rewarding.
- **Week 12**: Pre-4-month-regression sweet spot.
