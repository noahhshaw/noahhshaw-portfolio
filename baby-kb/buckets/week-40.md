# Week 40 — Days 273–279

## Focus
Beginning the final quarter. Walking is the dominant motor question now: ~50% of infants walk by 12 mo, ~75% by 14 mo, ~90% by 15 mo. The 75th-percentile CDC milestone for "walks alone" is now 15 mo (2022 revision). Whatever pace yours is on, daily practice + safe falls drives consolidation.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises; stands alone; possibly walks 1–5 steps.
- **Cognitive**: imitates more elaborate actions (stir with a spoon, push a toy car).
- **Language**: 1–3 specific words; gestures.
- **Social**: waves bye; plays pat-a-cake; gives objects on request.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Falls are routine. Bumps on carpet harmless.
- `[monitor]` Persistent inability to bear weight standing; persistent absence of imitation or babble.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness; new wheezing.
- `[call now]` Head injury with persistent vomiting/lethargy/loss of consciousness; severe respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **First-birthday RSVPs** going out 4–6 weeks pre-event.
- **Whole-milk introduction**: begin if planning. Watch for milk-protein issues (rare at this age — most cleared by 12 mo).
- **Preschool tours wrapping up** if ISAAGNY-style early-Jan deadlines.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Walking practice**: hold one hand outdoors. Push toy indoors.
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Begin to act out parts of stories.
- **First "art": crayon scribbling on paper. Triangle "first crayons" (Crayola My First) at 12+ mo, but mouth-safe alternatives at 10–11 mo.
