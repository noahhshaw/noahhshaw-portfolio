# Week 48 — Days 329–335

## Focus
First-birthday week. The 12-month visit may fall in this window depending on scheduling. Birthday party logistics dominate Saturday/Sunday; the rest of the week is normal operations.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks for many; standing alone for nearly all.
- **Cognitive**: tries to use objects correctly; imitates more elaborate actions.
- **Language**: 1–5+ words.
- **Social**: warm with familiar; wary of strangers.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Sugar response from cake (first major sugar exposure for many infants — short fussy crash possible).
- `[monitor]` Sleep disruption from family/travel.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Choking on cake (well-mashed is safest); severe respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **First-birthday party**.
- **Smash-cake photos** if doing.
- **Year-1 photo book ordering** from the year's photos.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **First-cake exposure** is its own enrichment.
- **Reading**: continue.
- **Walking outdoors**.

## What's coming next
- **Week 49**: Post-birthday.
- **Week 50**: Final pre-12-mo-visit if not done.
- **Week 51**: 12-mo visit final-window.
