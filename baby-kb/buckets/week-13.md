# Week 13 — Days 84–90

## Focus
End of the newborn period (the 0–3 month definition). Last week of "infant <90 days" febrile-protocol territory — fever thresholds become marginally less aggressive after day 90, but still consequential. The 4-month sleep regression is imminent.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: head fully steady when held upright; chest off floor in tummy time; rolling front-to-back possible.
- **Cognitive**: looks at toys, reaches purposefully, brings hands together.
- **Language**: cooing exchanges sustained; vocal play with squeaks and squeals.
- **Social**: smiles to get attention; reaches for familiar people.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` "Almost rolling" without success — endogenous progression continues regardless of practice volume.
- `[monitor]` No social smile yet (raise at 4-month visit). Sleep beginning to fragment (regression onset).
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent feeding refusal; new persistent crying pattern.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F rectal — under 90 days the workup-required threshold remains[1]. After day 91, AAP guideline shifts (more clinical-judgment-driven) but the practical default is still call same-day.

## Action items for parents
- **Schedule 4-month well visit** (window day 112–126).
- **Begin sleep environment optimization**: the 4-month regression coincides with the biological transition to adult-like sleep architecture — room-darkening blackout shades, white-noise machine (≤50 dB at the crib), 68–72°F room temperature[2].
- **Decide on sleep-training stance** before regression peaks. The decision is whether you'll sleep-train at 4 mo, 6 mo, or never. Decide as a couple — see `topics/sleep-training-methods.md` for evidence on each approach.
- **Bay Area / NYC families**: this is the right week to register for any waitlist-based "first" activities (Music Together, Gymboree, parent-baby yoga). They're not academic — but the logistics rehearsal matters more later.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 90 min/day cumulative; some infants now tolerate 15–20 min bouts.
- **Reading**: introduce one "concept" book per week — colors, shapes, animals. Repetition is still the vehicle.
- **Bath time as language lab**: narrate every step ("warm water, soap on your shoulders, lift your arm, splash") — you'll do bath nightly for 18+ months, the cumulative exposure is enormous.

## What's coming next
- **Week 14**: 4-month sleep regression often begins.
- **Week 15**: Regression continues; consider sleep-training onset.
- **Week 16**: 4-month well visit window opens.
