# Week 47 — Days 322–328

## Focus
Pre-birthday week. Logistics finalize: cake, decorations, family travel, the day-of timeline. The smash-cake photo is a tradition; a non-smash-cake photo session can also work for families who'd rather skip the food fight.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks for many; cruises for all.
- **Cognitive**: object permanence solid; cause-and-effect understanding deep.
- **Language**: 1–5+ words; rich gestural communication.
- **Social**: warm with familiar, wary with strangers.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Pre-event sleep disruption from family visiting.
- `[monitor]` Sleep regression that lasts >2 weeks.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Severe respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **Day-of timeline**: nap windows protected; party in late morning or after second nap.
- **Cake / smash-cake** picked up.
- **Photographer confirmed for the morning of.**

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: continue.
- **Walking practice**.
- **Music**.
