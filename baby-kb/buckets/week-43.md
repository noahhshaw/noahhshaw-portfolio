# Week 43 — Days 294–300

## Focus
Pre-12-month-visit window. Motor and language consolidation. The 12-mo visit is a major milestone visit — multiple vaccines, comprehensive surveillance, transition to year 2 anticipatory guidance.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks holding hands; stands alone; first independent steps for many.
- **Cognitive**: hides toys deliberately to recover; begins simple problem-solving.
- **Language**: 1–5+ specific words; vocalizations with adult-like intonation.
- **Social**: preference behaviors clear; engages in social games.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Sleep disruption around mobility surge.
- `[monitor]` No words by 12 mo + no babble + no joint attention combined → discuss seriously at 12-mo visit.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **12-mo visit prep finalized**: vaccines (MMR, VAR, HepA, PCV #4, Hib booster), hemoglobin, lead level, autism-screen-precursor.
- **Bottle-weaning** if not done: AAP recommends off bottle by 12–18 mo. Begin transition now if not started.
- **Birthday party logistics**: 2–4 weeks out depending on date.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day cumulative.
- **Walking-everywhere**: outdoor practice on grass, mulch, pavement, indoors carpet, hardwood.
- **Music**: continue.
