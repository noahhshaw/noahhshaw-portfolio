# Week 23 — Days 154–160

## Focus
Half-year mark approaching. Many practices schedule the 6-month visit this week. Solids may begin if you're starting at exactly 6 mo. RSV season is approaching for fall babies; for a 2026-05-11 baby, RSV season (Oct–Mar) hits at ~5–10 months — peak vulnerability for severe disease.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits with support, independently for some (CDC sits-without-support checkpoint: 9 months); pushes up with straight arms in tummy time.
- **Cognitive**: anticipates routines; begins to look for partially hidden objects.
- **Language**: takes turns vocalizing; makes squealing noises; varied babble.
- **Social**: laughs in response to play; recognizes familiar people.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Mild constipation when transitioning to solids — increase water (small offered cup) and fiber (pear, prune purée).
- `[monitor]` Persistent reflux + arching during feeds (consider GI consult).
- `[call within 24h]` First high-risk allergen reaction — rash beyond contact area, hives, vomiting within 2h of ingestion.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis post-allergen — facial swelling, breathing difficulty, lethargy, vomiting + hives — call 911. Always introduce allergens at home, in daytime, with epi-pen NOT routinely needed but pediatrician on speed dial.

## Action items for parents
- **6-month well visit attended.** Bring sleep log, feeding plan, allergen-introduction questions.
- **Vaccines today**: RV #3 (if RV5), DTaP #3, Hib #3 (if PRP-T), PCV #3, Influenza #1 (if Oct or later — annual flu vaccine starts at 6 mo).
- **First solid food**: if starting today, offer one new food at a time, 3-day gap before next new food (legacy advice; AAP no longer requires the 3-day rule, but it helps identify reactions).
- **Daycare illness plan refresh**: with RSV season approaching, refresh sick-policy. Some daycares require RSV-specific protocols.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **First mealtime**: place baby in high chair, family at table together. Even at 6 mo the family-meal context builds long-term eating norms (Druckerman, *Bringing Up Bébé*; also AAP).
- **Reading**: 30 min/day cumulative. Mix bedtime, bath-time, and one daytime read.
- **Music class**: enroll if not yet; weekly active music class at 6+ mo correlates with earlier music-cognition development[1].
