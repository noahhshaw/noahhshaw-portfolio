# Week 33 — Days 224–230

## Focus
Pre-9-month visit final prep. Pincer grasp emerging in many infants — practice with small soft foods (puffs, pieces of banana) drives refinement.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: pull-to-stand and cruising for early movers (CDC checkpoint: 12 months); rakes food with fingers (CDC 9-mo).
- **Cognitive**: looks for object you hide; bangs two objects together.
- **Language**: makes a lot of different sounds — "mamamama," "bababababa."
- **Social**: shy/clingy with strangers; reacts when you leave.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Increased clinginess; preference for one parent at a time.
- `[monitor]` Lack of joint attention; lack of varied babble.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; severe respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **9-mo visit prep notes**: language progress, sleep, feeding variety, any developmental concerns.
- **Iron-rich food check**: pediatrician will run hemoglobin. Iron-rich foods to feature: iron-fortified cereal, beef, lentils, beans, fortified pasta, dark leafy greens (well-cooked).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Pincer-grasp practice**: puffs, peas, small cheese cubes — sized just right for 2-finger pickup.
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. "Where's the…" + naming continues.
- **Music**: continue weekly class + daily live music.

- **Week 34**: 9-mo visit.
- **Week 35**: Post-visit review.
- **Week 36**: Pre-walking practice.
