# Week 21 — Days 140–146

## Focus
Pre-6-month consolidation. Sitting independent for short periods; first solids on the imminent horizon. Ear infections become more common from this age onward — peak incidence is 6 mo through 24 mo, often triggered by viral URIs.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: sits without support for 30+ sec; rolls to navigate.
- **Cognitive**: anticipates outcomes (smiles when bath water runs).
- **Language**: babble extending; turn-taking sustained for several exchanges.
- **Social**: clear preference for parents; smiles at familiar caregivers.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Drool surge ahead of first tooth (typically 6–10 mo).
- `[monitor]` Persistent ear-tugging without apparent illness — usually self-soothing, occasionally early ear pain.
- `[call within 24h]` Tugging ear + fever + fussiness — possible otitis media.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; persistent vomiting + reduced output.

## Action items for parents
- **6-month visit scheduling final check.**
- **Solids start equipment ready**: high chair, bibs, spoons, suction-cup bowl, small portion containers (2-oz silicone cups freeze well), cleanup mats under the chair.
- **Decide BLW vs purée vs combo** as primary approach. See `topics/introducing-solids.md`. Evidence-based summary: combo approaches are common and not harmful; BLW does not increase choking risk vs. purées per AAP-cited studies; allergen introduction matters more than method.
- **Plan first foods**: first 5 foods = avocado, banana, sweet potato, oatmeal cereal (iron-fortified), well-cooked egg yolk. Add peanut (thinned PB or Bamba puffs) within first 2 weeks of solids.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Sitting + book**: he can now sit propped to look at a book — increases engagement vs. supine reading.
- **Cause-and-effect toys**: a ball that rolls, a button-press musical toy. Beginning of intentional action-outcome learning.
- **Outdoor**: 30+ min outside daily. Texture (grass, sand), sound (birds, traffic, wind), light variation are all enrichment.

- **Week 23**: 6-month visit possibly happening.
- **Week 24**: Solids start.
