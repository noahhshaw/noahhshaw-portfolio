# Week 07 — Days 42–48

## Focus
Crying typically tapering. Sleep stretches at night sometimes lengthen to 4–5h. The 2-month vaccine visit is approaching. This is the last week of the "it's all just survival" phase for most families — the cognitive substrate for predictable patterns is coming online.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: head steady when held upright for short periods. Pushes up on forearms during tummy time.
- **Cognitive/sensory**: looks at toys briefly, follows objects with eyes.
- **Language**: cooing and gurgling getting more varied.
- **Social**: smiles when you talk to or smile at him (CDC 2-mo milestone).
- **Sleep**: longest stretch at night may be 4–5h for some infants, still 2–3h for others. Both are normal.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Drool starting (early salivary gland maturation, not necessarily teething); rolling-eye episodes during sleep transitions.
- `[monitor]` Persistent torticollis (head tilt to one side, limited rotation). Common after in-utero positioning; physical therapy referral at 2-mo visit if not resolving.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent vomiting >24h, refusal of multiple feeds in a row.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F rectal. Lethargy disproportionate to recent activity. Cyanosis.

## Action items for parents
- **2-month well visit** in the next 1–2 weeks. Review `calendars/well-visits.json` for the agenda. Bring feeding/output log.
- **Vaccine prep**: have infant Tylenol (acetaminophen) on hand — pediatrician will give weight-based dose. Plan for fussiness/low-grade fever 24–48h post-vaccine.
- **Daycare/nanny start date** confirmed; ideally a 3–5 day overlap with primary caregiver for transition.
- **First photo session planning**: if doing a "100-day photoshoot" (around day 100, week 14–15), book the photographer now. See `topics/photography-milestones.md`.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time** 30–45 min/day. Place high-contrast or simple-illustrated board books open in front of him during prone time.
- **Bath time as enrichment**: warm water, slow narration ("warm water, soap on your tummy, splash, splash"). Multi-sensory and routine-establishing.
- **Music**: introduce a "lights-out song" — same song every night before bed. The pairing is the active ingredient for sleep-onset association.

## What's coming next
- **Week 08**: 2-month well visit + vaccines.
- **Week 09**: Post-vaccine recovery; first social smiles consolidating.
- **Week 10**: Beginning to track objects with anticipation. First "almost laugh" sounds.
