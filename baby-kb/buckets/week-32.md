# Week 32 — Days 217–223

## Focus
8-month mark. Mobility now substantial — most infants are some combination of crawling, cruising, and pulling-to-stand. Joint attention emerging: he follows your gaze and your point.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: crawls reliably; cruises along furniture.
- **Cognitive**: looks for hidden objects; uses both hands for tasks.
- **Language**: imitates speech sounds; uses voice to get attention.
- **Social**: joint attention emerging — follows where you look or point.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Pulling up + falling = bumps and bruises. Falling in carpeted areas typically harmless.
- `[monitor]` No joint attention by 9–10 mo (failure to follow your gaze or pointing) — early flag for atypical development; raise at 9-mo visit.
- `[call within 24h]` New persistent illness; ear pulling + fever.
- `[call now]` Fall + loss of consciousness; severe respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **Joint attention exercise**: point at things and name them — "look, a *bird*." Pause for him to follow. This is a high-value daily ritual.
- **Pre-9-mo visit notes**: any concerns about development, sleep, feeding.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Start "where's the…" questions.
- **Naming routine**: name body parts in bath ("eyes, nose, mouth"). Even at 8 mo he's mapping receptive vocabulary.
- **Music**: live + recorded; if doing Suzuki, 30+ min listening daily.
