# Week 14 — Days 91–97

## Focus
4-month sleep regression onset for many infants. This is a *biological* transition — not a behavioral problem. Sleep cycles shorten from 50–60 minutes to ~45 minutes (adult-like), with waking between cycles. Most infants who weren't sleep-trained will begin waking 4–8 times per night for several weeks[1].

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: head fully steady; rolling front-to-back for some early rollers (CDC 2022 checkpoint: 6 months); pushing up on extended arms in tummy time.
- **Cognitive**: looks at hands with interest; opens mouth when he sees breast/bottle (CDC 4-mo).
- **Language**: cooing, "ah-goo," squealing.
- **Social**: smiles to get attention; chuckles in response to play.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Multiple night wakings starting week 14–18. Shorter naps. Distractible feeds (looking around, popping off the breast). All hallmarks of 4-month regression — biological, not behavioral.
- `[monitor]` Persistent feeding strike (day-time refusal); persistent middle-of-night-only feeds beyond ~6 mo (consider night-weaning then).
- `[call within 24h]` Acute fever >102°F. Persistent vomiting.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F under 90 days; ≥102°F at 90+ days; lethargy disproportionate to context.

## Action items for parents
- **Schedule 4-month well visit** if not done — window day 112–126.
- **Pre-regression sleep environment audit**:
  - Crib-only sleep (no Rock'n'Play, no Dock-a-Tot, no inclined sleeper — both are recalled or non-compliant with safe-sleep standards)[2].
  - Room ≤72°F.
  - Sleep sack instead of swaddle if he has shown any rolling — swaddling once rolling begins is unsafe.
  - White noise machine: ≤50 dB at the crib distance (Hugaboo, Hatch, Yogasleep Dohm — most have variable volumes).
- **Decide sleep-training approach** as a couple. See `topics/sleep-training-methods.md`. AAP, AASM, and Cochrane reviews support behavioral interventions from ~4–6 mo[3].

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 60–90 min/day still — though by now he may roll out of it spontaneously. Floor time on his back is also valuable.
- **Reading**: pause at images, name the object, point. Beginning of dialogic reading scaffold.
- **Object exploration**: hand him a teether or soft fabric book; observe him switching between hands. By 6 mo most infants transfer objects between hands; the precursor practice is now.

## What's coming next
- **Week 15**: Regression continuing for many; sleep-training decision point.
- **Week 16**: 4-month well visit + second vaccine round.
- **Week 17**: Post-vaccine recovery + regression possibly easing.
