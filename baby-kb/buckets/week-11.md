# Week 11 — Days 70–76

## Focus
The "easy stretch." For most families weeks 10–13 are the calmest of year 1. Sleep is consolidating. Smiles and laughs are reliable. Crying is well past peak. This is the right time to operationalize systems you'll need later: pumping if returning to work, sleep environment, household division-of-labor cadence.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: rolling occasionally (front-to-back tends to come first; CDC milestone for rolling moved to 6 months in 2022 revision but many infants do it earlier).
- **Cognitive**: anticipates routines (e.g., calms in bath setup before water touches him).
- **Language**: extended cooing exchanges; vocal range expanding.
- **Social**: chuckles or full laughs at peek-a-boo.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Sleep stretches lengthening then shortening week-to-week — normal variability.
- `[monitor]` Persistent asymmetric movement; persistent fisted hands without occasional opening.
- `[call within 24h]` Acute fever onset; new feeding refusal pattern.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F rectal under 90 days.

## Action items for parents
- **Pumping rhythm trial** if returning to work. Pump 1–2 sessions/day to build a freezer stash. AAP / La Leche guidance: 2 oz of frozen milk per missed feed is a good target.
- **Childcare backup plan**: who covers if the nanny is sick or daycare is closed? Identify a backup nanny agency or family-member-on-call before you need them.
- **Vaccinate caregivers**: confirm grandparents and any nanny are up to date on Tdap (adult pertussis booster), MMR, flu, COVID-19 per current CDC adult schedule. Pertussis ("cough of 100 days") is the most-likely-to-kill-an-infant vaccine-preventable illness from adult contacts.
- **First-birthday venue inquiry** (NYC/SF top venues book 6–9 mo out).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 60–75 min/day cumulative. Place an unbreakable mirror in front of him to motivate head lift.
- **Reading**: 15–20 min/day. Add one book with a repeated refrain ("Brown bear, brown bear, what do you see?"). Repeated-refrain books drive predictive language processing.
- **Music**: play one instrument's recording on repeat for 5–10 min during play (e.g., violin if you're considering Suzuki). Pre-Suzuki listening exposure starts at 6–12 mo per Suzuki Association[1]; you can begin earlier with no harm.

## What's coming next
- **Week 12**: First rolling for some infants. Easy stretch peak.
- **Week 13**: Quarter-year mark. End of newborn period.
- **Week 14**: Onset of the 4-month sleep regression for many.
