# Week 12 — Days 77–83

## Focus
End of the first quarter. For US families on standard 12-week leave, return-to-work week. The transition is logistically and emotionally significant. The 4-month sleep regression is approaching but not here yet (typically weeks 14–18).

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: rolling front-to-back possible; pushing up on extended arms during tummy time.
- **Cognitive**: visually tracks across full 180° smoothly.
- **Language**: prolonged cooing; first "raspberries" (lip vibrations).
- **Social**: laughing reliably; preferring familiar people; settling visibly when familiar voice enters the room.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Daycare/nanny adjustment fussiness for 1–2 weeks. Frequent illness in first 2 mo of daycare is normal — see `topics/daycare-illness-realities.md`.
- `[monitor]` Persistent crying disproportionate to handoff context. Sleep regression onset features (more frequent night wakings) — note when it starts.
- `[call within 24h]` First daycare fever ≥100.4°F (under 90 days, this still requires same-day evaluation).
- `[call now]` Fever ≥100.4°F under 90 days. Apnea or color change during sleep.

## Action items for parents
- **Daycare/nanny first day**: pack labeled bottles, extra outfits, diapers, sleep sack. Confirm communication channel (Brightwheel, daily text update, Slack channel — whatever the daycare uses).
- **Pumping schedule operational**: 3 sessions per 8h workday is typical; sessions of 15–20 min each. Refrigerate immediately, freeze same day if not using within 4 days.
- **Mother's pumping logistics**: insulated cooler bag, microwave-steam sterilizer bags for parts, spare pump parts in desk drawer.
- **Plan for the first daycare illness**: it will come within 2–4 weeks of starting. Have the pediatrician's after-hours line and a plan for who stays home.
- **First-birthday venue locked in** if booking required.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 75–90 min/day cumulative. Toys at varying distances motivate scooting/pivoting.
- **Reading**: continue daily. At daycare drop-off and pick-up, read the same book — bookends the day.
- **Object permanence pre-cursors**: hide a toy partially under a blanket; observe whether he reaches for the visible part. Full object permanence consolidates 8–10 mo.

## What's coming next
- **Week 13**: End of quarter 1. Stable rhythm or daycare-adjustment chaos — both common.
- **Week 14**: 4-month sleep regression onset for many.
- **Week 15**: Pre-4-month-visit prep.
