# Week 08 — Days 49–55

## Focus
The 2-month well visit and the first major vaccine round. This is the most consequential health-care touchpoint since the newborn visit. The vaccines are HepB #2, RV #1, DTaP #1, Hib #1, PCV #1, IPV #1 — six vaccines, typically delivered as 3–4 injections plus the oral rotavirus dose.

## Expected developmental milestones (CDC 2-month checklist)
- **Social/emotional**: calms when spoken to or picked up; looks at your face; smiles when you talk to or smile at him.
- **Language**: makes sounds other than crying; reacts to loud sounds.
- **Cognitive**: watches you as you move; looks at a toy for several seconds.
- **Motor**: holds head up briefly during tummy time; moves both arms and both legs; opens hands briefly[1].

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Mild fever (≤101°F) for 24–48h post-vaccine; redness/swelling at injection site; fussiness; sleeping more than usual; oral rotavirus dose may cause mild diarrhea or fussiness.
- `[monitor]` Fever 102–104°F post-vaccine. Persistent crying 1–3h post-vaccine. Hard injection-site swelling >2 inches.
- `[call within 24h]` Fever >104°F. Inconsolable crying >3h post-vaccine. Hypotonic-hyporesponsive episode (limp, pale, unresponsive) — rare but reportable.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis (facial swelling, breathing difficulty, hives) within 30 min of vaccine. Seizure activity. Encephalopathy signs. **Always go to ER for any fever ≥100.4°F rectal in infant <90 days, even immediately post-vaccine — pediatrician will not assume it's vaccine-related until other causes are ruled out**[2].

## Action items for parents
- **2-month well visit attended.** Bring printed list of questions; pediatrician visits run 20–30 min and time goes fast. Suggested questions:
  - When do we start sleep training (if at all)?
  - When do we plan to introduce solids (4 mo vs. 6 mo)?
  - What's our growth percentile trajectory?
  - When do we transition out of the swaddle?
- **Post-vaccine plan**: dose acetaminophen weight-based per pediatrician. Avoid prophylactic dosing before vaccines — some evidence it blunts antibody response[3]. Treat after, not before.
- **Pre-fill formula/breastmilk** for the next 24h — vaccine fussiness can disrupt feeding rhythm.
- **If returning to work soon**: trial run with daycare/nanny this week or next.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 45–60 min/day cumulative. Stagger across the day — 4–6 short bouts beat 1–2 long ones at this age.
- **Mirror play**: hold baby in front of mirror for 2–3 minutes. Self-recognition won't appear until ~18 mo, but face circuitry is calibrating.
- **Sing or play one specific song each diaper change**. The pairing ritual builds predictability and supports emotional regulation.

## What's coming next
- **Week 09**: Post-vaccine recovery week.
- **Week 10**: Look for the first laugh (typically 10–14 weeks).
- **Week 11**: Sleep stretches lengthening for many infants — pre-cursor to the 4-month regression.
