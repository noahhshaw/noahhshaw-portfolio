# Week 28 — Days 189–195

## Focus
Stranger anxiety in early peak. Object permanence consolidating — searching for hidden objects is emerging (solid by ~9 months). The cognitive substrate for "out of sight, out of mind" → "he still exists when I can't see him" is the same substrate driving separation distress.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: rocks on hands and knees; first quadrupedal crawl steps possible.
- **Cognitive**: looks for partially hidden objects; finding fully hidden objects emerging (≈9 months; object permanence, Piaget Stage 4).
- **Language**: extended babble; vocalizes when alone.
- **Social**: shows distress at strangers; may cry when held by unfamiliar adults.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Crying at unfamiliar people; clinging at daycare drop-off.
- `[monitor]` Sustained refusal of all unfamiliar caregivers (vs. typical 2–5 minute adjustment).
- `[call within 24h]` New illness onset.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **Daycare adjustment**: re-establish predictable handoff ritual (same goodbye phrase, same hug pattern, then exit decisively). Lingering goodbyes intensify distress.
- **Grandparent visits**: brief them — stranger anxiety is normal and not personal. 15 min of warm-up time before being held helps.
- **Sleep regression mini-window** possible at 8–10 mo (sometimes called the "8/9-month sleep regression"). It's tied to mobility surge and separation-anxiety cognition. Hold the line on sleep practices that have been working.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Peek-a-boo**: now genuinely fun and developmentally meaningful — exercises object permanence.
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Lift-the-flap books are at peak interest.
- **Crawling course**: pillow obstacles and supervised stair-climbing practice if you have stairs (always behind him to catch).

## What's coming next
- **Week 29**: Pre-9-mo. Stranger anxiety still peaked.
- **Week 30**: 9-mo visit window approaches.
- **Week 31**: Crawling consolidation.
