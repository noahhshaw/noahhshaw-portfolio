# Week 46 — Days 315–321

## Focus
Pre-12-month-visit final preparation. Some practices schedule the 12-mo visit in this window. The visit will include the major year-1 vaccine round (MMR, VAR, HepA + PCV #4 + Hib booster) and the transition to year 2 anticipatory guidance (whole milk, table foods, dental, walking, language).

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: walks 5+ steps for some (CDC walks-alone checkpoint: 15 months); stands alone momentarily for many (CDC checkpoint: 12 months).
- **Cognitive**: looks for fully hidden objects; uses tools to reach (drag a string, etc.).
- **Language**: first words for some (CDC 2022 checkpoint for words besides "mama"/"dada": 15 months); understands "no."
- **Social**: plays games; gives objects.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Mild post-vaccine fever 7–12 days after MMR (vaccine virus replication; not contagious).
- `[monitor]` No babble or words by 12 mo.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness; new wheezing.
- `[call now]` Anaphylaxis post-vaccine; fever ≥102°F + lethargy unrelated to vaccine; respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **12-mo visit prep**: written question list. Vaccines acknowledged. Sleep status. Eating range. Walking.
- **MMR-related caution**: live-attenuated vaccine; mild fever and rash 7–12 days post-vaccine common (not contagious). Avoid pregnant household contacts for MMR/VAR briefly per pediatrician guidance.
- **Bottle weaning** in progress.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day.
- **Walking practice**.
- **Music**.
