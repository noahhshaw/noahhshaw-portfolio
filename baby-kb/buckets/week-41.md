# Week 41 — Days 280–286

## Focus
Pre-birthday quarter mid-stretch. Self-feeding more refined; eating most family foods (soft, appropriately sized); drinking from cup with help. Words may be 1–5 by now or zero — both within range.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises fluently; first steps for many.
- **Cognitive**: tries to imitate adult tasks (sweeping with toy broom).
- **Language**: 1–5 specific words; gestures rich.
- **Social**: emotional expressions varied; preferences for foods and people clear.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Picky days (refuses previously-eaten foods); separation distress at parent-leaving.
- `[monitor]` No words by 12 mo (raise at 12-mo visit, but this alone is not a strict red flag — 25% of 12-mo-olds aren't using words).
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness; new ear pulling + fever (otitis media common).
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F + lethargy; respiratory distress.

## Action items for parents
- **Birthday cake/smash-cake plan**: smash-cake photos are a tradition; book photographer if doing.
- **Family travel coordination** if grandparents traveling for the birthday.
- **First-shoes** purchased for outdoor walking.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day. Shorter and more interactive at this age — books with flaps, textures, mirrors.
- **Walking practice**: every day, indoors and outside.
- **Singing with motion**: "If You're Happy and You Know It" — imitate clapping, stomping.
