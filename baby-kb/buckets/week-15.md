# Week 15 — Days 98–104

## Focus
Mid-regression for many. The "100-day mark" is a culturally celebrated milestone in Korean (백일, *baekil*) and Chinese (百日宴) traditions; among Bay Area / NYC families with East Asian heritage or aesthetic sensibility, the 100-day photo session is standard. Logistically, this is also the typical onset window for sleep training if you're going to do it early.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: pushing up on extended arms; rolling more reliably; bearing weight on legs when held standing.
- **Cognitive**: tracks objects in motion smoothly; reaches for objects within range.
- **Language**: extended cooing turns; consonant-vowel combinations multiplying.
- **Social**: smiles spontaneously; laughs; preference for familiar people becoming sharper.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Continued night wakings. Catnaps (30–45 min). Distractible feeding.
- `[monitor]` Worsening pattern beyond regression baseline — every-30-min wakings, total night sleep <8h, persistent inability to nap outside motion.
- `[call within 24h]` Acute illness signs.
- `[call now]` Fever ≥102°F at 90+ days; lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **100-day photo session** if doing one. Bring 2–3 outfit changes; plan for 60–90 min total session including soothing breaks.
- **Sleep-training decision point**: by week 15–16, have a written plan if you're starting at 4 mo. Methods compared in `topics/sleep-training-methods.md`. The most-evidence method is graduated extinction (Ferber); the fastest-acting is unmodified extinction (Weissbluth); the gentlest are the chair method and pick-up-put-down. None has been shown to cause measurable harm at 5-yr follow-up[1].
- **4-month visit prep**: bring sleep log, feeding log, any concerns about milestones.

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Tummy time**: 60–75 min/day. By now he is likely rotating on his belly (pivoting).
- **Reading**: introduce books with simple flap-lift mechanics (*Where Is Baby's Belly Button?*). Anticipation of the lift drives fine-motor reaching.
- **Music**: try a structured class (Music Together, Kindermusik) if logistics allow. Active participation > passive listening for early music-cognition outcomes[2].

## What's coming next
- **Week 16**: 4-month well visit window.
- **Week 17**: Vaccines. Post-vaccine recovery.
- **Week 18**: Regression often easing as new sleep architecture stabilizes.
