# Week 39 — Days 266–272

## Focus
Last week of the third quarter. Three months until the first birthday. He's likely cruising fluidly, possibly taking first independent steps, vocalizing with intent, and feeding himself with hands and pre-loaded utensils.

## Expected developmental milestones
- **Motor**: cruises; stands alone briefly; some first independent steps.
- **Cognitive**: object permanence solid; functional use of objects.
- **Language**: first specific words emerging for some (CDC 2022 places first words besides "mama"/"dada" at the 15-month checkpoint); gestures (waves bye, shakes head).
- **Social**: gives object on request; engages in social games.

## Watch-fors
- `[low concern]` Mild bumps from falls; food refusal of previously-loved foods (taste preferences shifting).
- `[monitor]` No first words; no varied babble; no joint attention.
- `[call within 24h]` Persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Severe respiratory distress; fever ≥102°F + lethargy.

## Action items for parents
- **First-birthday venue confirmation** if not done. Cake order placed (most bakeries need 2–3 weeks lead).
- **Whole-milk transition planning**: AAP recommends transition from formula or breast milk to whole cow's milk at 12 mo. Start mixing 25/75, then 50/50, etc., over a week or two if anticipating resistance.
- **Preschool applications submitted** if ISAAGNY (deadline Jan 8 for 2s programs).

## Enrichment opportunities
- **Reading**: 30+ min/day; rotate one new book per week.
- **First puzzle**: 1-piece, large, knob-handled; matching shape.
- **Music**: continue.
