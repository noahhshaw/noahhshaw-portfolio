# Activity Pipeline — Year-1 to Year-3 Waitlist Reality

## Why this matters in year 1

Many top NYC and SF Bay Area enrichment programs (music, swim, gym, foreign-language preschool, eventual instrumental teachers) have 6–24 month waitlists. The decisions made in year 1 — especially months 6–12 — set the year 2–3 trajectory. Missing the application window often means alternative-path or year-delay.

This is the high-achievement parents' equivalent of capacity planning. Demand for top programs exceeds supply. Treat applications as research-driven and time-sensitive.

## By region

### NYC

**Music classes (newborn to age 5)**:
- **Music Together** (multiple locations) — quarterly enrollment; rolling waitlist for prime time slots.
- **92Y Music School Suzuki program** — early childhood music classes from age 2; formal Suzuki lessons by age 3–4. Apply 6–12 months before desired start.
- **Diller-Quaile** — early childhood music; competitive admission for ages 2+.
- **Mannes Prep** (New School) — by audition age 5+.

**Gym/movement**:
- **Apple Seeds** (Tribeca/Westchester) — drop-in and class structure; quarterly enrollment.
- **Asphalt Green AquaKidz** — swim from 3 mo; waitlist 3–6 mo for prime times.
- **Local Gymboree, Lil Sportsters**.

**Languages**:
- **Lyceum Kennedy** — French immersion preschool from age 2.
- **Spanish American Institute, Mandarin Together** — language playgroups.
- **Apply 12+ months ahead** for ages 2+ language immersion.

**Preschool 2s**:
- ISAAGNY-affiliated preschools (~50 schools): apply by **January 8** for fall-of-year-baby-turns-2 entry.
- Tour calendars open in **September** of the previous year. Tour 8–12 schools.
- Standardized notification: **late February**.

### San Francisco Bay Area

**Music**:
- **San Francisco Conservatory of Music — Adult Extension Division Suzuki** (and similar programs at Crowden, Music in the Schools).
- **Hello Music** (Suzuki violin/piano in SF/Marin).
- **Music Together** — multiple SF locations.
- **Crowden Music Center** (Berkeley) — pre-Suzuki and Suzuki programs.

**Gym/movement**:
- **My Gym** (multiple Bay Area locations).
- **Studio Grow** (East Bay).
- **Little Gym** (Peninsula).
- **Janet Pomeroy Center** (SF, swim from 3 mo).

**Languages**:
- **Le Petit Baleen** (French) — from age 2.
- **Mandarin Immersion Parents Council** programs.
- **Aha! Bilingual** — Spanish immersion preschool age 2+.

**Preschool 2s**:
- ISSFBA-affiliated schools: apply typically by **January–February** for fall entry.
- SFUSD pre-K (subsidized): SFUSD enrollment portal opens in fall; deadlines vary year-to-year.
- Top private programs (Live Oak, Town School, Synergy, Marin Country Day Preschool, Hamlin, Cathedral): **January deadlines, March notification, April reply.**

## Timing by month-of-baby's-life

| Baby's age | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0–6 mo | **Music**: enroll in Music Together once baby is 6+ weeks. **Suzuki listening recordings** start at 6 mo if instrument decided. **Preschool research**: read about NYC ISAAGNY / SF ISSFBA process. |
| 6–9 mo | **Gym**: enroll in age-appropriate gym/movement class (My Gym, Apple Seeds). **Swim**: enroll in parent-baby swim class (some accept 3 mo, most 6+ mo). **Suzuki teacher waitlist**: identify and join waitlists for top teachers; lessons begin age 3–4 typically. |
| 9–12 mo | **Preschool list-building**: tour 5–10 schools (NYC: ISAAGNY directory; SF: ISSFBA directory). **Music Together**: continue. **Mandarin/Spanish** language playgroups if planning bilingual. |
| 12–18 mo | **Preschool applications**: prepare by November of year baby turns ~17–22 mo (ISAAGNY deadline early January, the year baby turns 2). |
| 18–24 mo | **Preschool decisions**: notification February, reply March, start September. |

## Cost expectations

- **Music Together**: $200–300 per 10-week semester.
- **Gym/movement classes**: $400–800 per semester / 10–15 classes.
- **Swim class**: $400–800 per semester.
- **Mandarin/Spanish playgroup**: $300–800 per semester.
- **Preschool 2s tuition (top NYC private)**: $40,000–60,000/year (5-day program).
- **Preschool 2s tuition (top SF private)**: $30,000–50,000/year.
- **Suzuki violin lessons (when starting age 3–4)**: $80–150/lesson, ~30/year + group class = $3,500–8,000/year.

## Application-prep needs (preschool)

For ISAAGNY/ISSFBA-affiliated 2s programs:
- **Application form** — varies by school.
- **Application fees** — $50–100/school × ~10 schools.
- **Parent essay / questionnaire** — 1–3 pages per school.
- **Tour attendance** — typically required.
- **Interview** — for some 2s programs (more common for 3s+).
- **Recommendations** — sometimes required (less common for 2s than 3s+).
- **Financial information** — for need-based aid.

## Operational discipline

This is the kind of project that requires a tracking spreadsheet:
- School name, region, deadline, tour date, decision date, fit notes, cost.
- Application status by deadline.
- Reply by date.

Treat it like investment-committee work: build the universe, narrow the watchlist, do the diligence, decide.

## What NOT to do

- **Wait until the toddler stage** — if you start the preschool process at 18 mo for a 24-mo entry, top programs are full.
- **Over-enroll** — 2–3 enrichment activities is plenty in year 1; 5+ produces overstimulation and parental burnout.
- **Treat as resume-building** — at year 1, this is exposure and routine-building, not credentialing. The credential value is years away.
