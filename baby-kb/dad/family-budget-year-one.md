# Family Budget — Year One

## The number

Year-1 incremental cost for a Bay Area / NYC dual-earner couple, before college savings: **$45,000–$95,000** above pre-baby baseline. Major drivers: childcare, healthcare, gear, food, services. Geography swings the range substantially.

## Line items

### Childcare (the dominant line)

- **Nanny full-time**: $65,000–110,000/year (Bay Area / NYC; lower in other metros).
- **Daycare full-time**: $24,000–48,000/year ($36,000–60,000 for top-tier NYC/SF).
- **Au pair**: $20,000–28,000 + room/board.
- **Nanny share**: $35,000–55,000.

If both parents take 12-week leave, childcare starts ~week 12 — annual cost prorated to ~75% of full-year.

### Healthcare

- **OB / delivery**: covered by insurance; out-of-pocket typically $1,000–5,000 (deductible + coinsurance).
- **Pediatric well visits + sick visits**: covered by insurance; copays ~$0–50/visit. Year-1 visits: ~10–15.
- **Prescriptions**: ~$200–500/year typical.
- **Insurance premium increase**: adding a child to family plan typically $300–800/month. Compare employer-plan options.
- **Lactation consultant**: $100–300/visit; some insurance covers under ACA.

### Gear (one-time year 1)

- **Crib, mattress, sheets**: $300–700.
- **Stroller**: $200–1,500 (UPPAbaby Vista on the high end; Babyzen Yoyo for compact).
- **Car seat (infant)**: $200–500.
- **Car seat (convertible, used by year 1)**: $200–500.
- **Pack-n-play**: $100–250.
- **Bassinet**: $200–1,500 (SNOO if elected).
- **High chair**: $30 (IKEA) to $300 (Stokke Tripp Trapp).
- **Carrier**: $80–250.
- **Bottles, breast pump (insurance-covered), nursing supplies**: $200–500.
- **Diaper changing setup**: $100–300.
- **Clothing**: $500–2,000 (highly variable).
- **Toys/books**: $200–500 in year 1; rotation strategy controls volume.

**Total gear**: $2,000–8,000.

### Diapers and consumables

- **Diapers**: ~$80–120/month for 2,500–3,500 disposable diapers/year. ~$1,000–1,500 year 1.
- **Wipes**: ~$30–50/month; ~$400–600 year 1.
- **Formula** (if applicable): $1,800–2,400/year (standard formula); $4,000+ for hypoallergenic.
- **Solids** (from 6 mo): $40–80/month; $300–600 in year 1.

### Services

- **Postpartum doula** (optional): $40–80/hour; $1,500–4,000 typical engagement.
- **Night nurse** (optional): $250–600/night; rare but exists for high-need families.
- **House cleaning**: $200–400/month (newly justified post-baby).
- **Photography** (newborn, 100-day, 6-month, 1-year): $1,300–4,800/year.
- **Music classes**: $250 × 4 sessions = $1,000/year.
- **Baby/parent yoga, swim**: $300–800/year.

### Healthcare-adjacent

- **Life insurance** (new term policies for both parents): $720–2,000/year (combined).
- **Estate plan setup** (one-time): $2,500–6,000.

### College savings

- **529 plan contributions**: $0–$24,000+/year (varies dramatically by family strategy).
- See `dad/finances-529.md` for benchmarks.

## Tax-advantaged offsets

- **Child Tax Credit**: $2,000/year (current law; $1,700 refundable). Phases out at AGI $200K single / $400K married.
- **Dependent Care FSA**: $5,000/year pre-tax (use-it-or-lose-it; covers childcare).
- **HSA**: if eligible high-deductible plan, $4,300 individual / $8,550 family contribution limits 2026.
- **Child and Dependent Care Tax Credit**: 20–35% of up to $3,000 (one child).
- **Adoption Credit** (if adopting): up to $17,280/child 2026.

## Insurance considerations

- **30-day enrollment window** — must add baby to health insurance within 30 days of birth.
- **HSA contribution adjustment** — eligibility may shift with family-status change.
- **FSA election change** — qualifying life event allows mid-year change.

## Where to economize without regret

- **Used gear** for short-life items (bassinet, infant car seat that's outgrown by 9 mo). Buy new only safety-critical items (CPSC standards, no recall, no expired car seat).
- **Library books** instead of buying.
- **Hand-me-down clothes** — baby outgrows 0–6 mo sizes in weeks.
- **Generic formula** if formula-feeding (Costco Kirkland Signature is identical to brand-name on protein/macronutrient profile).
- **DIY puréed solids** vs. pouches (food processor + freezer cubes; 90% cost savings).

## Where to NOT economize

- **Car seat**: new only, current safety standards.
- **Crib**: new or post-2011 (no drop-side), current sleep-safety compliance.
- **Pediatrician**: choose based on quality, not insurance-driven default.
- **Sleep environment** (room-darkening, white noise) — small money, big sleep ROI.
- **Estate planning** — DIY is the worst-place-to-economize.
