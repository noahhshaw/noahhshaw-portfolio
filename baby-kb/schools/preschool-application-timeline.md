# Preschool Application Timeline (NYC, SF Bay Area)

## Why this matters in year 1

Top private preschools in NYC and SF Bay Area have application deadlines, tour calendars, and notification schedules that require parents to begin research roughly 18 months before desired start. For a 2026-05-11 baby:

- **2s program (entering September 2028)**: applications submitted **November 2027 – January 2028**. Tours **September–November 2027**. Decisions **February 2028**.
- **Preschool research starts in earnest**: months 6–12 (year 1).

This is not college-application-level effort, but it is more involved than most parents expect.

## NYC — ISAAGNY-affiliated preschools

### Standardized calendar (ISAAGNY)

For 2s/3s/4s programs at independent schools:

- **Tour calendars open**: September of the year before entry (so for September 2028 entry, tours open September 2027).
- **Application deadline**: typically **January 8** for 2s (variable for older grades, often early–mid January). Confirm per school.
- **Early notification opt-in window**: opens November of application year (per ISAAGNY 2025–26 guidance).
- **Standardized notification date**: late February of admission year (e.g., February 26, 2026 for the current cycle).
- **Reply date**: early March (e.g., March 5, 2026).

Schools may require:
- Application form + fee ($50–100/school).
- Parent essay (1–3 pages).
- Tour attendance.
- Sometimes recommendation letter from a current/former family.
- Sometimes interview (more common for 3s and 4s).
- Sometimes child observation (less common for 2s).

### Strategy

1. **Tour 8–12 schools** in fall 2027 (tour calendar window).
2. **Narrow to 5–8** for application.
3. **Apply by January 8, 2028** to all of them.
4. **Wait for late-February notification**.
5. **Reply by early March**.

Most families end up with 1–3 acceptances; a small fraction get nothing in the first round and pursue rolling-admissions or alternate paths. This is not failure; it's volume of applications.

### Cost

- **Application fees**: $50–100 × 8–12 schools = $400–1,200.
- **Tuition (2s, 5-day program, top schools)**: $40,000–60,000/year 2026 dollars.
- **Tuition (2s, 3-day program)**: $20,000–35,000/year.
- **Financial aid**: most schools have aid, varying generosity. Aid applications typically through SSS or School and Student Services portal.

## San Francisco Bay Area — ISSFBA-affiliated preschools

### Standardized calendar (ISSFBA)

Similar structure to NYC but slightly different dates:

- **Tours**: October–December of year before entry.
- **Application deadline**: typically **January–February** (varies more than NYC).
- **Notification**: **March 10–16** (varies by school).
- **Reply by**: **March 16–20** typical.

### Top SF Bay Area preschools (sample)

- The Hamlin School preschool, Live Oak, Town School (boys), Cathedral, Synergy, Marin Country Day Preschool, Chinese American International School (CAIS), French American International School, Hoover, Marin Horizon Preschool, Mountain View Academy.
- East Bay: Park Day School, Aurora School, Tehiyah Day School preschool.
- Peninsula: Castilleja preschool (girls), Phillips Brooks School preschool, Woodland School.
- Mission Bay School (new, opening 2026 per SFUSD).

### SFUSD pre-K (subsidized public)

- **PreKindergarten Program**: ages 2y9m–5y, full-time and school-day, subsidized + tuition slots.
- **Application portal**: SFUSD Schools Enrollment portal; opens fall.
- **Waitlist visibility**: March 16, 2026 (per current cycle); seat eligibility April 20, 2026.
- **Free option for working families** at certain income thresholds.

## Strategy by age

### Months 6–12 (your year 1)

- **Read about the process**: ISAAGNY website, Parents League of New York website (newyorkfamily.com guides), ISSFBA.
- **Build a long list of 12–20 schools** based on geography, philosophy, gender (some single-sex), religion, schedule.
- **Visit playgrounds + open houses casually** — many schools have community days.
- **Talk to current parents** through your social network.

### Months 13–24 (year 2 of life)

- **Tour intentionally** in fall (year 2 fall = fall 2027 for September 2028 entry).
- **Narrow to 8–10 application targets**.
- **Prepare application materials**: parent essays, supporting docs.
- **Recommendations**: identify who can write if needed (rare for 2s but common 3s+).

### Months 18–22 (early year 2)

- **Submit applications** (deadline early January of year 2).

### Months 22–24

- **Wait and reply** — mid–late February 2028 notifications, March reply.

## What schools are looking for

For 2s programs:
- **Family fit** with school philosophy.
- **Demonstrated investment in process** (showed up to tours, complete application).
- **Reasonable parent expectations** (not the next Harvard).
- **Stable home environment**.

For 3s+ programs:
- All of the above PLUS:
- **Child observation** in some cases (for atypical-development concerns or specific schools).
- **Academic readiness for 4s** if the school has academic-track expectations.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't tour 50 schools** — tour shows up on records, schools talk; over-applying signals lack of seriousness.
- **Don't pay for "preschool admissions consultants"** unless your geography/network is exhausted; the process is straightforward enough to navigate independently.
- **Don't rely on legacy alone** — even at schools with sibling/legacy preference, this is one input among many.
- **Don't lie or embellish** essays — admissions directors can detect.

## Backup plans

- **Rolling admissions**: many smaller programs have year-round availability. Less prestigious but often perfectly-good environments.
- **Co-op preschools**: parent-run, lower cost, requires parent volunteer hours.
- **Religious-affiliated**: church- and synagogue-attached preschools often have rolling admission.
- **Wait a year**: enter as a 3s rather than 2s. Many top schools have larger 3s classes than 2s.
