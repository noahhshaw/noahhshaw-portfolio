# Travel with Infant

## When is the easy window?

For most families, **6–9 months** is the sweet spot for travel — pre-mobility, post-major-vaccines, pre-stranger-anxiety-peak. Before 4 months, travel is feasible but high-overhead (sleep environment recreation hard). After 12 months, walking + tantrum risk + needing snacks/activities multiplies logistics.

## Air travel

### Documents

- **Domestic**: birth certificate sometimes requested at gate (rare); usually no ID for under-2.
- **International**: passport. First-time infant passport requires both parents present in person; 6–12 weeks processing; expedited 2–3 weeks.
- **Visas**: per destination country.

### Booking

- **Lap infant**: free domestic, 10% of fare on international (under 2). Higher-risk: turbulence, no FAA-approved restraint.
- **Own seat with FAA-approved car seat**: full child fare; safest. Recommended for any flight >2h, all international.
- **Bulkhead row**: extra floor space, possible bassinet (long-haul). Book early; airlines hold bulkhead seats for families.
- **Short layovers**: avoid <90 min — feed/diaper changes inflate transit time.
- **Red-eye vs daytime**: red-eyes can sync with bedtime sleep but recovery on landing is hard. Daytime flights with cooperative naps are a gamble.

### Day-of

- **Early to airport**: 2.5h domestic, 3.5h international. Stroller through TSA; collapse at gate. Wear baby through security.
- **Feed during takeoff and landing**: helps with ear pressure equalization.
- **Diaper change in lavatory** (most have changing tables) or at gate before boarding.
- **Pack 4–6 hours of supplies** in carry-on regardless of flight length (delays).

### Carry-on essentials

- Diapers (2× expected need).
- Wipes (1.5× expected).
- 1–2 changes of clothes for baby; 1 spare top for parent.
- Bottles + formula (pre-measured) or pumping setup.
- Snacks if 6+ mo.
- Quiet toys (1–2).
- Pacifiers (with clip).
- Burp cloths (3+).
- Plastic bags for soiled items.
- Acetaminophen, thermometer.
- Blanket/swaddle.
- Travel-size hand sanitizer.

### Checked

- **Stroller and car seat** check free at gate (not counted as bag).
- **Travel crib**: pack-n-play or BabyBjörn travel crib.
- **Sleep environment**: Slumberpod (blackout tent for pack-n-play), white noise machine.

## Road trips

### Car seat

- Maintain rear-facing for year 1 + (AAP recommends until age 2+).
- Don't add aftermarket products (head supports, harness covers) not approved by manufacturer — interferes with tested crash performance.
- Check installation before departure (CPST or fire station).

### Stops

- Plan a stop every 2 hours minimum: feed, diaper, weight-bearing time.
- Avoid overnight driving with infant — schedule over 2 days for >8h trips.
- **Never leave baby in parked car** even briefly — temperature rise is fast and lethal.

## Hotel / lodging

- **Travel crib** preferred over hotel-supplied crib (variable quality, recall status unknown).
- **Slumberpod or blackout sheet** for room-darkening.
- **White noise machine** packed.
- **Childproofing kit**: outlet covers (4–6), corner pads (4), drawer locks (2). Travel-size baby-proofing.
- **Confirm crib in advance** — many hotels charge or have limited supply.

## International travel

- **Time zone management**: try to put baby on local time within 48h. Outdoor light exposure morning of arrival; expose to local meal times.
- **Vaccinations**: depending on destination, additional vaccines (yellow fever for some Africa/Latin America destinations from 9 mo). CDC Travel Health Notices.
- **Insurance**: travel medical insurance covering infant. Confirm pediatric coverage at destination.
- **Bring**: doses of acetaminophen, ibuprofen (6+ mo), thermometer, pediatrician contact info, summary of vaccinations and any health conditions.

## Specific logistics

### Pumping while traveling

- TSA allows breast milk in carry-on (not subject to 3.4-oz liquid limit). Notify at security; may swab.
- Cooler with ice packs (also TSA-allowed).
- Pump on plane in seat with cover; in airport in mother's room or family bathroom.
- Hotel: confirm fridge in room.

### Food and water

- **Bottled water for formula** in international destinations with non-potable tap.
- **Fresh ingredients vs. packed pouches**: pouches travel; fresh available locally typically.
- **Common problem**: limited highchairs in some restaurants/cultures — bring foldable option (Inglesina Fast Table Chair clamps to table).

### Illness while traveling

- **Pediatrician contact**: call your home pediatrician via portal; they can advise.
- **Local pediatrician/ER**: identify before departure.
- **Travel insurance**: confirm coverage.

## Watch-fors

- `[low concern]` Sleep disruption 1–3 days into trip; brief loose stool from new foods.
- `[monitor]` Persistent fussiness post-flight (pressure-related); persistent sleep disruption >5 days.
- `[call within 24h]` Fever ≥100.4°F under 90 days while traveling; persistent illness.
- `[call now]` Severe respiratory distress; persistent vomiting + dehydration; suspected ear infection from flight + severe pain.
