# Photography — Milestone Schedule

## What to capture and when

### Newborn (days 0–14)

- **Hospital photos**: most hospitals offer a paid photographer; quality varies.
- **Home newborn shoot** (days 5–14): professional photographer, ~90 min, posed and candid. Best in this window — baby still curls naturally, sleeps deeply.
- **Cost**: $400–1,500.
- **Tip**: book during pregnancy; top photographers fully booked 6 weeks out.

### 100-day (days ~95–105)

Cultural milestone in Korean (백일, *baekil*) and Chinese (百日) traditions; widely adopted aesthetically by Bay Area / NYC families. Baby is now smiling, head-controlled, and not yet rolling out of poses.

- **Studio or in-home**: 60–90 min.
- **Cost**: $300–1,000.
- **Tip**: feeds and naps on schedule pre-shoot. Bring 2–3 outfit changes.

### Monthly stack (days 1, 30, 60, …, 365)

DIY tradition: photo against fixed backdrop, fixed pose, fixed time-of-day. The 12-up frame at 1 year is the most-popular family-album item.

- **Setup**: same blanket or backdrop. Same room. Same outfit family or sticker indicating month.
- **Time**: ~5 min/month if set up well.
- **Frame at year 1**: 12-up grid in matted frame. Multiple custom-print services; Artifact Uprising is the high-end pick.

### 6-month sit-up

- Solo sit on a blanket on the floor; first time sitting independently.
- Multiple poses with parent close for safety.
- **Cost**: $200–800 if professional; trivial if DIY.

### First birthday + cake smash

See `topics/first-birthday-planning.md`. Smash cake is the core image. Family portrait separately.

### First steps / first words

Spontaneous; have phone ready in months 9–14.

## Daily / weekly photos

Phone photos are ample. Curate quarterly into an album (Google Photos, Apple Photos, dedicated child-tracking apps like Tinybeans, Frameo for grandparents).

## Family album setup

- **Cloud sync**: shared album with grandparents (Apple Family Sharing / Google Photos shared albums).
- **Tinybeans / family-only social app**: for daily photo sharing without exposing baby publicly.
- **Hard archive**: external drive backup quarterly. iCloud/Google Drive is fine but not eternal.
- **Year-1 photo book**: 60–100 pages compiled at month 13–14.

## Privacy

The default for many parents is no public social media of baby. Reasoning: she'll have her own choice about her digital footprint at age 13+; preserving it requires you not to have populated the internet first. Tinybeans, family iMessage threads, and grandparent-only Frameo digital frames cover the audience without public exposure.

|---|---|
| Newborn pro shoot | $400–1,500 |
| 100-day pro shoot | $300–1,000 |
| 6-month milestone | $200–800 |
| 1st birthday + cake smash | $400–1,500 |
| Monthly DIY stack | $50–200 (printing) |
| Year-1 photo book | $80–200 |

Total professional photography year 1: $1,300–4,800.

- `[low concern]` Posed shoots interrupted by feeds, blowouts, fussiness — par for the course.
