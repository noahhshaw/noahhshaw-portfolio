# Postpartum Physical Recovery

## What to expect — week by week

### Days 0–6 (lochia rubra)

- **Bleeding**: heavy, bright red. Soak a thick maxi pad every 2–3 hours typical first 1–3 days, tapering through day 6. Clots up to plum-size are normal; passing a fist-sized clot is reportable.
- **Perineum** (vaginal birth): swollen, sore. Peri bottle, ice packs (first 24–48h), sitz baths after 24h. Stitches dissolve over 2–3 weeks if any.
- **Cesarean incision** (if applicable): tender, immobile-feeling. Ambulation as soon as possible to reduce DVT risk; use abdominal pillow when coughing/laughing for 1–2 weeks.
- **Afterpains**: uterus contracting back; worse with breastfeeding; intensify with subsequent births. Ibuprofen.
- **Constipation**: fear of bearing-down combined with iron supplementation + dehydration. Stool softener (Colace), water, fiber.
- **Hemorrhoids**: common. Witch hazel pads (Tucks), warm sitz baths, topical hydrocortisone if pediatrician/OB-okayed.

### Days 7–14 (lochia rubra → serosa)

- **Bleeding**: pink-brown, less heavy. Pads still daily.
- **Perineum**: most of acute soreness gone by end of week 2 for first-degree tears.
- **C-section incision**: external sutures or staples removed week 1–2 if used; steri-strips may stay 2 weeks. Itching = healing.
- **Sleep deprivation**: peak. Plan night-shift split with partner.

### Weeks 3–4

- **Bleeding** mostly stopped; lochia alba (yellowish-white, light) days 10–15+.
- **Energy** beginning to return.
- **Mood**: baby blues should be resolving. Persistence beyond week 2 raises postpartum depression concern; see `mom/ppd-watch-fors.md`.

### Weeks 5–6

- **6-week postpartum visit** with OB. Pelvic exam, mood screen, contraception, exercise return clearance.
- **First period** (if bottle-feeding) typically returns 6–10 weeks. Breastfeeding suppresses for varying duration (months to year+).
- **Sex**: most OBs clear after 6 weeks. Many women aren't ready emotionally or physically — that's fine. Vaginal dryness from breastfeeding-related estrogen suppression is common; lubricant helps.

### Weeks 7–12

- **Pelvic floor**: weakness, occasional stress incontinence common. **Pelvic floor PT** referral is appropriate at the 6-week visit if any issues.
- **Diastasis recti** (abdominal separation): check yourself or have PT check; >2-finger separation benefits from targeted PT.
- **Hair**: shedding begins ~3 mo postpartum (rebound from pregnancy hair retention). Resolves 6–12 mo.

### Months 3–6

- **Energy** approaching pre-pregnancy levels for most.
- **Body composition**: weight loss plateaus; gradual return depends on activity, breastfeeding, sleep.
- **Sleep**: still fragmented if not sleep-trained.

### Months 6–12

- **Stable** — most pre-pregnancy capacities returning.
- **Pelvic floor PT** continued benefit if any residual symptoms.

## Operational basics

### Postpartum hospital bag / home setup

Have these in place before delivery:
- Peri bottle (the hospital usually provides; restock).
- Maxi pads (overnight thickness; 3–4 packs).
- Mesh underwear (hospital provides; supplement).
- Adult diapers (Depend) for first 3–4 days — easier than pads.
- Stool softener (Colace).
- Acetaminophen + ibuprofen alternating per OB.
- Nipple cream (lanolin or Silverette cups).
- Breast pads (disposable or washable).
- Comfortable nursing bras + sleep bras.
- Sitz bath if perineal repair.
- Witch hazel pads (Tucks).
- Hemorrhoid cream.
- Peri-cleanse spray (Frida Mom).

### Movement

- **Days 0–7**: gentle walking. No prolonged lifting or core work.
- **Weeks 2–6**: continued walking, light stretching. No high-impact, no abdominal work.
- **Week 6+ (post-clearance)**: gradual return to activity. Walking → swimming → light strength → high-impact. Pelvic floor PT-guided if any symptoms.
- **C-section recovery is longer**: 8–10 weeks before any abdominal-focused work; deferential to surgical site.

### Nutrition

- **Breastfeeding adds ~400–500 kcal/day** to maintenance. Do not under-eat to lose weight; supply tanks.
- **Iron + calcium + DHA**: continue prenatal vitamin through breastfeeding period.
- **Hydration**: ~1 oz/lb body weight is reasonable while breastfeeding.

### Sleep

- **Realistic target year 1**: 5–7 hours total in 24h, fragmented.
- **Strategies**:
  - Nap when baby naps (the cliché has merit).
  - Night shift split with partner.
  - Sleep training when developmentally appropriate (4+ mo) per `topics/sleep-training-methods.md`.
  - Caffeine timing: morning + early afternoon only; avoid after 14:00 to preserve nap-when-baby-naps capacity.

## Intimacy and contraception

- Most OBs clear sex at 6 weeks postpartum. Many women aren't ready until 8–12 weeks.
- Vaginal dryness from estrogen suppression while breastfeeding — lubricant.
- Contraception: discuss at 6-week visit. Options: progestin-only (mini-pill, IUD, implant) compatible with breastfeeding; combined estrogen products usually deferred until weaning.

## Watch-fors

- `[low concern]` Lochia variations (heavier some days), sweat changes, hair shedding at 3 mo.
- `[monitor]` Lochia alba persisting >6 weeks; intermittent reappearance of red bleeding for a day or two.
- `[call within 24h]` Heavy bleeding (soaking >1 pad/hour for >2 hr); fever ≥100.4°F; foul-smelling lochia (endometritis); incision redness/discharge; calf pain (DVT); chest pain or shortness of breath (PE — emergency).
- `[call now]` Heavy bleeding with clots + lightheadedness (postpartum hemorrhage); fever + severe abdominal pain; chest pain or shortness of breath; severe headache + visual changes (preeclampsia can present postpartum); thoughts of harming self or baby (see `mom/ppd-watch-fors.md`).
